The '''2020–21 Eredivisie''' was the 65th season of the Eredivisie since its establishment in 1955. The season began on 12 September 2020 and concluded on 16 May 2021.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.vi.nl/nieuws/plan-van-der-sar-krijgt-vorm-eredivisie-toppers-in-tweede-seizoenshelft|title=Plan Van der Sar krijgt vorm: Eredivisie-toppers in tweede seizoenshelft|publisher=[[Voetbal International]]|language=nl|date=22 June 2020|access-date=23 June 2020}}</ref>

On 2 February 2021, the [[Royal Dutch Football Association|KNVB]] announced that effective immediately clubs would be allowed to make an extra substitution for suspected concussions as part of an [[International Football Association Board|IFAB]] sanctioned trial. This trial will allow teams to make up to six substitutions across four breaks in the match instead of five substitutions across three breaks in the match.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://nos.nl/artikel/2367070-knvb-start-proef-met-extra-wissel-bij-vermoedelijke-hersenschudding.html|title= KNVB start proef met extra wissel bij vermoedelijke hersenschudding |publisher=[[Nederlandse Omroep Stichting|NOS]]|language=nl|date=2 February 2021|access-date=3 February 2021}}</ref>

[[AFC Ajax|Ajax]], having won the title in [[2018-19 Eredivisie|2018–19]], were the title holders, since the [[2019–20 Eredivisie|2019–20 edition]] was cancelled due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic in the Netherlands]] after 25  matchdays and the title was not awarded.

[[AFC Ajax|Ajax]] successfully defended their title, becoming the champions in round 31 after beating [[FC Emmen|Emmen]] 4–0 in their own [[Johan Cruyff Arena]] in [[Amsterdam]] on 2 May 2021.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-05-02 |title= On This Day {{ ! }} Ajax verovert 35e landstitel|url=https://www.ajax.nl/artikelen/on-this-day-ajax-verovert-35e-landstitel#:~:text=Exact%20%C3%A9%C3%A9n%20jaar%20geleden%2C%20op,Devyne%20Rensch%20en%20Davy%20Klaassen. |access-date=2026-03-15 |website=Ajax |language=NL}}</ref>

== Teams ==
A total of 18 teams are taking part in the league. There were no changes in the league's makeup due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic in the Netherlands|COVID-19 pandemic]].

== Play-offs ==
For both play-offs, who plays who, is not decided based on draws, but the brackets are fully preset based on seeds. The seeds are assigned based on the final ranking after the regular season. The best ranked team will get the highest seed (lowest number). Eredivisie teams are considered to be better ranked than eerste divisie teams.

Contrary to previous years, all rounds are no longer two legs (home and away) but a single leg. The team with the best seed will get the home advantage.

If a match is leveled at the end of the normal playing time, extra time will be played (two periods of 15 minutes each) and followed, if necessary, by a penalty shoot-out to determine the winners.

=== European competition play-offs ===
The four best ranked teams without a European tournament ticket will play for one spot in the [[2021–22 UEFA Europa Conference League qualifying phase and play-off round#Second qualifying round|2021–22 UEFA Europa Conference League second qualifying round]]. The highest seeded team will always play at home.
