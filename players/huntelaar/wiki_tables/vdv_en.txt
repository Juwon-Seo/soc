The '''{{lang|de|Vereinigung der Vertragsfußballspieler e.&nbsp;V.|italics=no}}''' ('''VDV''') is the professional footballer's union in Germany. As of 2026, it has approximately 1400 members playing in the German professional leagues.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2 June 2026 |title=Michael Olise & Vincent Kompany win German Players' Union awards, eight Bayern players in Team of the Season |url=https://fcbayern.com/en/news/2026/06/michael-olise-vincent-kompany-win-german-players-union-awards-eight-bayern-players-in-team-of-the-season |access-date=9 September 2026 |archive-date=15 June 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260615054750/https://fcbayern.com/en/news/2026/06/michael-olise-vincent-kompany-win-german-players-union-awards-eight-bayern-players-in-team-of-the-season |url-status=live }}</ref> Junior footballers at the beginning of their professional careers can also be members from the age of fifteen. Footballers who have been playing abroad or have retired can remain members.

== History ==
The union was founded on 15 June 1987 in [[Offenbach am Main]] by the former professional footballers [[Benno Möhlmann]], [[Ewald Lienen]] and [[Frank Pagelsdorf]], together with colleagues including [[Guido Buchwald]], [[Florian Gothe]], [[Charly Körbel]] and [[Bruno Labbadia]]. The goal of the union was to work in the interest of the players. Especially important at that time were work and contract conditions, the independence of players, and the democratisation of German professional football. In general, the goal is to give footballers a common voice towards clubs and associations.

== Presidents ==

* 1987–1992: [[Benno Möhlmann]]
* 1992–1994: Stefan Lottermann
* 1994–1996 [[Jürgen Rollmann]]
* 1996–1997 Benno Möhlmann{{efn|Möhlmann was later named honorary president of the VDV.}}
* 1997–1999: [[Jürgen Sparwasser]]
* 1999–present: [[Florian Gothe]]

== Awards ==
Voted by professional footballers in the union, the VDV Silver Boot is awarded to the best player of the season, the VDV Silver Arrow is awarded to the best newcomer of the season, while the VDV Silver Bench is awarded to the best coach of the season.<ref name="2025/26" />
