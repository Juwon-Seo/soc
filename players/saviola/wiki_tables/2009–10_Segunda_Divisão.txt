The '''2009–10 [[Segunda Divisão]]''' season was the 76th season of the competition and the 63rd season of recognised third-tier [[Association football|football]] in [[Portugal]].<ref name="Soccer Library">{{cite web|url=http://soccerlibrary.free.fr/por_ft.pdf|title=Portugal - Table of Honor - soccerlibrary.free.fr|work=Soccer Library|access-date=2012-05-02|archive-date=2019-10-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191010211315/http://soccerlibrary.free.fr/por_ft.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> [[C.D. Fátima|Fátima]] were the defending champions.

The league was contested by 47 teams in 3 divisions with [[FC Arouca]], [[Moreirense FC]] and [[União da Madeira]] winning the respective divisional competitions and the former two teams gaining promotion to the [[Liga de Honra]].  The overall championship was won by [[FC Arouca]].<ref name="Soccer Library"/>

==Play-offs==
The play-off for [[Liga de Honra]], the teams play each other in a home and away round robin. However, they do not all start with 0 points. Instead, a weighting system applies to the teams standing at the start of the play-off mini-league.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.fpf.pt/portal/page/portal/PORTAL_FUTEBOL/COMPETICOES/NOTICIA?notid=9092644 |title=The play-off for Liga de Honra |access-date=2018-07-28 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120320031136/http://www.fpf.pt/portal/page/portal/PORTAL_FUTEBOL/COMPETICOES/NOTICIA?notid=9092644 |archive-date=2012-03-20 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
