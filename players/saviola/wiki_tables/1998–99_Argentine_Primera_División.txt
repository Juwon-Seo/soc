The '''1998–99 [[Argentine Primera División]]''' was the 108th season of top-flight [[football in Argentina]]. The season ran from August 7, 1998, to June 21, 1999.<ref name=rsssf>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesa/arg99.html Argentina 1998–99] by Javier Romiser on RSSSF.com</ref> Both main clubs from Córdoba, [[Talleres de Córdoba|Talleres]] (champion of [[1997–98 Primera B Nacional]]) and [[Club Atlético Belgrano|Belgrano]] (winner of "Torneo Reducido", after beating [[Aldosivi]] in a two-legged series)<ref>[https://www.belgranocordoba.com/noticia-belgrano/1037/ ¡A 17 años del ascenso del '98!] on Belgrano website</ref> were promoted from [[Primera B Nacional]].<ref>Argentina: 1ra. "B" Nacional AFA 1997/98 - Etapa Campeonato - Zona "A" by José Carluccio, 28 Oct 2012</ref>

[[Boca Juniors]] won both, Apertura and Clausura championships (a total of 24 league titles to date), while [[Club Atlético Platense|Platense]] and [[Club Atlético Huracán|Huracán]] were relegated with the worst points averages.<ref>"Argentina: 1ra. División AFA - Torneo Apertura 1998" by José Carluccio, 24 Nov 2012</ref>
