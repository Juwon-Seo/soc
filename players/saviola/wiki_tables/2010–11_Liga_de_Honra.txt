The '''2010–11 [[Liga de Honra]]''' season was the 21st season of the second-tier football in [[Portugal]]. A total of 16 teams will contest the league, 12 of which already contested in the [[2009–10 Liga de Honra|2009–10]], and two of which were promoted from the [[Portuguese Second Division]], and two of which were relegated from [[2009–10 Portuguese Liga|2009–10]]. The official ball is [[Adidas Jabulani]].
