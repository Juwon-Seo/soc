The '''2000–01 [[Argentine Primera División]]''' was the 110th season of top-flight [[football in Argentina]]. The season ran from July 28, 2000 to July 11, 2001.<ref name=rsssf>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesa/arg01.html Argentina 2000–01] by Javier Roimiser on RSSSF.com</ref>

[[Boca Juniors]] won the Apertura (its 25th league title) and [[San Lorenzo de Almagro|San Lorenzo]] the Clausura (13th title) championships, while [[Club Atlético Los Andes|Los Andes]] and [[Club Almagro|Almagro]] were relegated after spending only one season in the highest division.

=== Promotion Playoffs ===
[[Club Atlético Belgrano|Belgrano (Córdoba)]] and [[Argentinos Juniors]] (3rd. and 4th. worst averages) played the [[promotion and relegation|promotion]] playoff v [[Quilmes Atlético Club|Quilmes]]<ref>[https://www.lanacion.com.ar/deportes/futbol/quilmes-gano-y-sostiene-la-ambicion-del-ascenso-nid312552/ Quilmes ganó y sostiene la ambición del ascenso] on ''La Nación'', 13 June 2001</ref> and [[Instituto Atlético Central Córdoba|Instituto (C)]] (qualified from [[2000–01 Primera B Nacional]]) respectively,<ref>[https://www.lavoz.com.ar/deportes/futbol/instituto-con-buenas-malas-promociones-revalidas/ Instituto con buenas y malas] on ''La Voz''</Ref> in a [[two-legged tie|two-legged]] series. 

Although the two playoff series ended 1–1 on aggregate, Argentinos and Belgrano remained in the top division due to rules stated that in cases like those, teams in Primera División would be declared winners (sporting advantage).<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20211023183050/https://www.lavoz.com.ar/deportes/futbol/julio-mugnaini-la-historia-de-un-gol-historico-en-belgrano-quilmes-y-un-dia-del-padre-especia/ Julio Mugnaini, la historia de un gol histórico], La Voz</ref><ref>"Argentina: 1ra. División AFA - Promoción 2001" by José Carluccio, 8 Feb 2013</Ref>
