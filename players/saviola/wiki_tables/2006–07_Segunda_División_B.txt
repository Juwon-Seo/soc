The '''Segunda División B 2006–07''' season was the 30th since its establishment. The first matches of the season were played on 27 August 2006, and the season ended on 24 June 2007 with the [[2006–07 Segunda División Play-Off|promotion play-off finals]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.flashscores.co.uk/football/spain/segunda-division-b-play-out-2006-2007/|title=Segunda Division B Play Out 2006/2007 Results, Football Spain - FlashScores.co.uk|website=www.flashscores.co.uk|language=en-GB|access-date=2019-03-08}}</ref>
