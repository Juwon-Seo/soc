The '''2009 [[Belarusian Premier League]]''' was the 19th season of top-tier [[Association football|football]] in [[Belarus]]. It started on April 4 and ended on November 8, 2009. [[FC BATE Borisov|BATE Borisov]] were the defending champions.

==Team changes from 2008 season==
Due to league reduction from 16 to 14 teams three relegated teams ([[FC SKVICH Minsk|Lokomotiv Minsk]], [[FC Savit Mogilev|Savit Mogilev]] and [[FC Darida Minsk Raion|Darida Minsk Raion]], who finished 14th, 15th and 16th in 2008 respectively) were replaced by only one team, the winner of [[2008 Belarusian First League|2008 First League]] [[FC Minsk|Minsk]].

==Overview==
[[FC BATE Borisov|BATE Borisov]] won their 6th champions title and qualified for the next season's Champions League. The championship runners-up [[FC Dinamo Minsk|Dinamo Minsk]], bronze medalists [[FC Dnepr Mogilev|Dnepr Mogilev]] and yet to be determined 2009-10 Cup winners qualified for the Europa League. Due to decision to gradually reduce Premiere League to 12 clubs (14 in 2009, 12 in 2010) three lowest placed teams ([[FC Gomel|Gomel]], [[FC Granit Mikashevichi|Granit Mikashevichi]]<br>[[FC Smorgon|Smorgon]]) relegated to the First League.
