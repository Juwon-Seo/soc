The '''2011–12 Primeira Liga''' (also known as '''Liga ZON Sagres''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 78th season of the [[Primeira Liga]], the top professional league for Portuguese [[association football]] clubs. It began on 14 August 2011 and ended on 13 May 2012.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.lpfp.pt/pages/calendario.aspx?jornada=30|title=Calendário|trans-title=Calendar|language=pt|work=LPFP|date=29 June 2011|access-date=15 December 2011}}</ref> A total of 16 teams contested the league, 14 of which already took part in the [[2010–11 Primeira Liga|previous season]] and two of which were promoted from the [[Liga de Honra]]. [[FC Porto|Porto]] were the defending champions and secured their 26th and second consecutive league title. [[Óscar Cardozo]] and [[Lima (footballer, born 1983)|Lima]], respectively [[S.L. Benfica|Benfica]]'s and [[S.C. Braga|Braga]]'s strikers, were the joint top scorers with 20 goals.

==Teams==
Sixteen teams contested the league, fourteen of which already contested in the [[2010–11 Primeira Liga|2010–11 season]] and two of which were promoted from the [[2010–11 Liga de Honra]]. The two teams relegated following the 2010–11 season were [[Portimonense S.C.|Portimonense]], which returned to the Liga de Honra just a year after promotion, and [[Associação Naval 1º de Maio|Naval]], returning to the second tier after a six-year stay. Replacing them in the top flight were Liga de Honra champions [[Gil Vicente F.C.|Gil Vicente]], competing in their 14th Liga season after returning from a five-year absence, and [[C.D. Feirense|Feirense]], who were in the top division for the fourth time and the first since the [[1989–90 Primeira Liga|1989–90 season]].

=== Annual awards ===
==== Portuguese Golden Ball ====
The [[Portuguese Golden Ball]] was given to [[James Rodríguez]], the youngest player (21) to ever receive the award.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.fifa.com/worldcup/preliminaries/news/newsid=2039441/index.html?intcmp=newsreader_news_box_1|title=South American hopefuls enter final straight |website=FIFA.com|date=21 March 2013|access-date=15 July 2026}}</ref>

==== LPFP Primeira Liga Player of the Year ====
The LPFP Primeira Liga Player of the Year was awarded to [[Hulk (footballer)|Hulk]]. He became the first player to win the award twice.<ref name="1LPFP12">{{cite web | url= http://www.abola.pt/nnh/ver.aspx?id=340395 | title= Hulk conquista prémio de melhor jogador da Liga 2011/12 | trans-title= Hulk wins award for best player in the League 2011/12 | publisher= [[A Bola]] | language= pt | date= 5 July 2012 | access-date= 5 July 2012 | archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20120712155323/http://www.abola.pt/nnh/ver.aspx?id=340395 | archive-date= 12 July 2012 | url-status= dead | df= dmy-all }}</ref>

==== LPFP Primeira Liga Breakthrough Player of the Year ====
The LPFP Primeira Liga Breakthrough Player of the Year was awarded to [[James Rodríguez]].<ref name="1LPFP12"/>

==== LPFP Primeira Liga Goalkeeper of the Year ====
The LPFP Primeira Liga Goalkeeper of the Year was awarded to [[Rui Patrício]].<ref name="1LPFP12"/>

==== LPFP Primeira Liga Manager of the Year ====
The LPFP Primeira Liga Coach of the Year was awarded to [[Vítor Pereira (footballer, born 1968)|Vítor Pereira]].<ref name="1LPFP12"/>

==== LPFP Primeira Liga Fairplay Award ====
The LPFP Primeira Liga Fairplay Award was awarded to [[Rio Ave F.C.|Rio Ave]].<ref name="1LPFP12"/>
