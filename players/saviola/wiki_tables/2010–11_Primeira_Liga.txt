The '''2010–11 Primeira Liga''' (also known as '''Liga ZON Sagres''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 77th season of the [[Primeira Liga]], the top professional league for Portuguese [[association football]] clubs. It began on 15 August 2010 and ended on 14 May 2011. A total of 16 teams contested the league, 14 of which already took part in the [[2009–10 Primeira Liga|previous season]] and two of which were promoted from the [[Liga de Honra]]. [[S.L. Benfica|Benfica]] were the defending champions but finished runners-up to [[FC Porto|Porto]], who won their 25th league title in the club's first-ever [[Perfect season#Association football|unbeaten season]]. Porto's forward [[Hulk (footballer)|Hulk]] was the top scorer with 23 goals.

==Teams==
[[C.F. Os Belenenses|Belenenses]] and [[Leixões S.C.|Leixões]] were relegated at the end of the [[2009–10 Portuguese Liga|2009–10 season]] after finishing in the bottom two places of the table. Belenenses ended an 11-year spell at the highest level of [[Portugal|Portuguese]] football,<ref>{{cite news |title=Belenenses relegation |url=https://sol.sapo.pt/PaginaInicial/Desporto/Interior.aspx?content_id=170609 |work=Sol |publisher=sol.sapo.pt |date=27 April 2010 }}{{Dead link|date=June 2022 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> while Leixões returned to the [[Liga de Honra]] after three years.<ref>{{cite news |title=Leixoes to relegation to the secondary level |url=http://www.maisfutebol.iol.pt/fcporto/benfica-braga-liga-leixoes/1159596-1304.html |work=MaisFutebol |publisher=maisfutebol.iol.pt |date=2 May 2010 |access-date=15 August 2010 |archive-date=29 March 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120329042157/http://www.maisfutebol.iol.pt/fcporto/benfica-braga-liga-leixoes/1159596-1304.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The two relegated teams were replaced by Liga de Honra champions [[S.C. Beira-Mar|Beira-Mar]] and runners-up [[Portimonense S.C.|Portimonense]]. Beira-Mar returned to the top-level league after three years of absence,<ref>{{cite news |title=The Beira-Mar is champion Liga de Honra! |url=https://economico.sapo.pt/noticias/beira-mar-e-portimonense-sobem-a-i-liga_89101.html |work=Económico |publisher=economico.sapo.pt |date=8 May 2010 |access-date=28 July 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304083846/http://economico.sapo.pt/noticias/beira-mar-e-portimonense-sobem-a-i-liga_89101.html |archive-date=4 March 2016 |url-status=dead}}</ref> and Portimonense made their first appearance in their league since being relegated at the end of the [[1989–90 Portuguese Liga|1989–90 season]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Portimonense returns to Portuguese Liga |url=http://www.rr.pt/bolabranca_detalhe.aspx?fid=73&did=103303 |work=RR |publisher=rr.pt |date=8 May 2010 }}</ref>
