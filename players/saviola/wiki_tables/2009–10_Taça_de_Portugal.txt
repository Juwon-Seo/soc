The '''2009–10 Taça de Portugal''' was the 70th season of the [[Taça de Portugal]]. The competition began on 30 August 2009, with the first round matches, and concluded with the final, held on 16 May at the [[Estádio Nacional]], in [[Oeiras Municipality, Portugal|Oeiras]], between defending champions [[FC Porto|Porto]] and then [[Liga de Honra]] team [[G.D. Chaves|Chaves]]. Porto won 2–1 to take their second consecutive cup, and assuring a place in [[UEFA Europa League 2010–11#Play-off round|play-off round]] of the [[2010–11 UEFA Europa League]].
