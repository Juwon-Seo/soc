The '''2014 Liga Super''' ({{langx|en|'''2014 Super League'''}}), also known as the '''Astro Liga Super''' for sponsorship reasons, is the 11th season of the [[Liga Super]], the top-tier professional [[association football|football]] league in Malaysia.<ref name="LS2014">[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesm/malay2014.html] 2014 Liga Super</ref>

The season was held from 17 January and concluded on 25 June 2014.<ref name="LS2014"/>

The [[Liga Super]] champions for 2014 was [[Johor Darul Ta'zim F.C.|Johor Darul Ta'zim]] and this season was the start of them winning the league every season until today.<ref name="LS2014"/>

==Major changes==

The following are the key changes of 2014 Liga Super season as compared to the previous season:

* The 2014 [[Piala Sumbangsih]] match between [[LionsXII]] and [[Pahang FA|Pahang]] will also be a Liga Super match. Results of the 90-minute match will be counted towards the league table classification. The last time this happened was during the [[2011 Liga Super]] season.
* Goal-line referees will be introduced in selected matches during the season, and will be fully implemented for all matches in the [[Liga Super|2015 Liga Super]] season if the feedback is positive.<ref>{{cite news |title=Goal line technology |url=http://www.nst.com.my/sports/soccer/goal-line-referees-to-make-debut-in-m-league-1.411229 |work=New Straits Times |date=25 November 2013 |access-date=25 November 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131125094221/http://www.nst.com.my/sports/soccer/goal-line-referees-to-make-debut-in-m-league-1.411229 |archive-date=25 November 2013 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
* A total of 4 foreign players can be registered by Liga Super teams, including at least one player from [[Asian Football Confederation|AFC]] countries. A maximum of 3 foreign players can be fielded at one time in a match. The announcement was made by FAM during the exco meeting in November 2013, following a decision to upgrade the foreign players quota from 2 in 2013 to 3 in the April 2013 meeting.<ref>{{cite news |title=Goal line technology}}</ref>

==Teams==
A total of 12 teams compete in the 2014 season which includes the top 10 teams that participated in the [[2013 Liga Super|2013 season]] and champions and runners-up of the [[2013 Liga Premier]].

[[Felda United F.C.|Felda United]] and [[Negeri Sembilan FA|Negeri Sembilan]] were relegated at the end of the [[2013 Liga Super]] season after finishing in the bottom place of the league table.

[[2012 Liga Premier]] unbeaten champions [[Sarawak FA|Sarawak]] and runners-up [[Sime Darby FC|Sime Darby]] secured direct promotion to the Liga Super.
