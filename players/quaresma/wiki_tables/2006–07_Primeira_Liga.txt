The '''2006–07 Primeira Liga''' (also known as '''BWINLIGA''' for sponsorship reasons) was the [[Primeira Liga#All-time Primeira Liga table|73rd edition]] of top flight of Portuguese football. It started on 27 August 2006 with a match between [[Vitória F.C.|Vitória de Setúbal]] and [[Associação Académica de Coimbra – O.A.F.|Académica]] and ended on 20 May 2007. The league was contested by 16 clubs, with [[FC Porto|Porto]] as defending champions.

Porto and [[Sporting CP]] were both qualified for the [[2007–08 UEFA Champions League]] group stage, while Benfica qualified for the UEFA Champions League qualifying round. [[S.C. Braga|Braga]], [[C.F. Os Belenenses|Belenenses]] and [[F.C. Paços de Ferreira|Paços de Ferreira]] qualified for the [[2007–08 UEFA Cup]]. [[S.C. Beira-Mar|Beira-Mar]] and [[C.D. Aves|Desportivo das Aves]] were relegated to the [[Liga de Honra]]. [[Liédson]] was the top scorer with 15 goals.

== Promotion and relegation ==

=== Teams relegated to [[Liga de Honra]] ===
*[[F.C. Penafiel|Penafiel]]
*[[Rio Ave F.C.|Rio Ave]]
*[[Vitória S.C.|Vitória de Guimarães]]
*[[Gil Vicente F.C.|Gil Vicente]]

The professional competitions shrunk from 18 to 16 clubs following last season's promotions and relegations' modifications. [[F.C. Penafiel|Penafiel]], [[Rio Ave F.C.|Rio Ave]], [[Vitória S.C.|Vitória de Guimarães]] were relegated to the Liga de Honra.

In June 2006, [[C.F. Os Belenenses|Belenenses]], who finished 15th in the previous season, were scheduled to be relegated. However, the Lisbon team presented a complaint to the [[Portuguese League for Professional Football]] regarding the use of a non-registered player ([[Mateus Galiano da Costa|Mateus]]) by 13th-placed [[Gil Vicente F.C.|Gil Vicente]]. The penalty pretended by Belenenses would result in Gil Vicente being relegated in their place. This claim generated a series of appeals and controversial statements to the media. The decision has been taken on 23 August and made Belenenses stay in the Portuguese Liga while Gil Vicente were relegated to the Liga de Honra.
