The '''2001–02 [[Segunda Divisão]]''' season was the 68th season of the competition and the 55th season of recognised third-tier [[Association football|football]] in Portugal.<ref name="Soccer Library">{{cite web|url=http://soccerlibrary.free.fr/por_ft.pdf|title=Portugal – Table of Honor – soccerlibrary.free.fr|work=Soccer Library|access-date=2012-05-16|archive-date=2019-10-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191010211315/http://soccerlibrary.free.fr/por_ft.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Overview==
The league was contested by 60 teams in 3 divisions with [[SC Covilhã]], [[FC Marco]] and [[União Funchal]] winning the respective divisional competitions and gaining promotion to the [[Liga de Honra]].  The overall championship was won by [[SC Covilhã]].<ref name="Soccer Library"/>
