The '''2002–03 [[Segunda Divisão]]''' season was the 69th season of the competition and the 56th season of recognised third-tier [[Association football|football]] in Portugal.<ref name="Soccer Library">{{cite web|url=http://soccerlibrary.free.fr/por_ft.pdf|title=Portugal – Table of Honor – soccerlibrary.free.fr|work=Soccer Library|access-date=2012-05-16|archive-date=2019-10-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191010211315/http://soccerlibrary.free.fr/por_ft.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Overview==
The league was contested by 59 teams in 3 divisions with [[CD Feirense]], [[Leixões SC]] and [[GD Estoril Praia]] winning the respective divisional competitions and gaining promotion to the [[Liga de Honra]].  The overall championship was won by [[Leixões SC]].<ref name="Soccer Library"/>
