The '''2011–12 Israeli Premier League''' was the thirteenth season since its introduction in 1999 and the 70th season of top-tier football in [[Israel]]. It began on 20 August 2011 and ended on 12 May 2012.<ref>{{cite news |title=The Israeli Premier League will start on 20 August and will end on 12 May 2012 |url=http://www.one.co.il/Article/182243.html |publisher=. ONE |date=29 June 2011 |access-date=29 June 2011 |language=he}}</ref> [[Maccabi Haifa F.C.|Maccabi Haifa]] were the defending champions.

[[Hapoel Ironi Kiryat Shmona F.C.|Ironi Kiryat Shmona]] secured the title with a 0–0 draw against [[Hapoel Tel Aviv F.C.|Hapoel Tel Aviv]] on 2 April 2012. This was their first Israeli league title, This draw gave Ironi Kiryat Shmona a 16-point advantage over the second-place team Hapoel Tel Aviv with five more rounds to go.

==Structural changes==
There was three structural changes:<ref>{{cite news |title=No longer for halving of points |url=http://www.one.co.il/Article/181742.html |publisher=. ONE |date=20 June 2011 |access-date=20 June 2011 |language=he}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=This season will have no halving of points |url=http://www.one.co.il/Article/182085.html |publisher=. ONE |date=27 June 2011 |access-date=27 June 2011 |language=he}}</ref>
*The middle playoff was cancelled, with only top and bottom playoff to be contested by eight teams each, according to their regular season placement. with each team plays 37 matches.
*The points were no longer halved after the regular season.
*There will be three relegated teams, and only one promoted team from [[Liga Leumit]].

A total of sixteen teams are competing in the league, including fourteen sides from the [[2010–11 Israeli Premier League|2010–11 season]] and two promoted teams from the [[2010–11 Liga Leumit]].

[[Hapoel Ashkelon F.C.|Hapoel Ashkelon]] and [[Hapoel Ramat Gan Giv'atayim F.C.|Hapoel Ramat Gan]] were directly relegated to the [[2011–12 Liga Leumit]] after finishing the [[2010–11 Israeli Premier League|2010–11 season]] in the bottom two places.

Two teams were directly promoted from the [[2010–11 Liga Leumit]]. These were champions [[Ironi Nir Ramat HaSharon F.C.|Ironi Ramat HaSharon]] and the runners-up [[Hapoel Rishon LeZion F.C.|Hapoel Rishon LeZion]].
