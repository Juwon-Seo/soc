The '''2005–06 Primeira Liga''' was the [[Primeira Liga#All-time Primeira Liga table|72nd edition]] of top flight of Portuguese football. It started on 19 August 2005 with a match between [[Sporting CP]] and [[C.F. Os Belenenses|Belenenses]] and ended on 7 May 2006. The league was contested by 18 clubs with [[S.L. Benfica|Benfica]] as defending champions. 

[[FC Porto|Porto]] and [[Sporting CP]] were both qualified for the [[UEFA Champions League 2005-06|2005–06 UEFA Champions League]] group stage, and Benfica qualified for the UEFA Champions League qualifying round; [[S.C. Braga|Braga]], [[C.D. Nacional|Nacional]] and [[Vitória F.C.|Vitória de Setúbal]] qualified for the [[2006–07 UEFA Cup]]; in opposite, with the league dropping to 16 teams, four teams were relegated to the [[Liga de Honra]]; [[Gil Vicente F.C.|Gil Vicente]], [[Rio Ave F.C.|Rio Ave]], [[Vitória S.C.|Vitória de Guimarães]] and [[F.C. Penafiel|Penafiel]]. [[Albert Meyong|Meyong]] was the top scorer with 17 goals.

The season's first goal was scored by [[Rogério Fidélis Régis|Rogério]], who scored a 39th-minute goal for Sporting against Belenenses. The first yellow card of the season was given to Sporting's [[Fábio Rochemback]] in the opening game of the season, and the first red card was given to Benfica's [[João Pereira (footballer, born 1984)|João Pereira]] in his club's away draw against [[Associação Académica de Coimbra – O.A.F.|Académica de Coimbra]].

==Promotion and relegation==

===Teams relegated to [[Liga de Honra]]===
*[[Moreirense F.C.|Moreirense]]
*[[S.C. Beira-Mar|Beira-Mar]]
*[[G.D. Estoril Praia|Estoril]]

Moreirense, Beira-Mar and Estoril were relegated to the Liga de Honra following their final classification in [[2004–05 Primeira Liga|2004–05 season]].

===Teams promoted from Liga de Honra===
*[[C.F. Estrela da Amadora (1932)|Estrela da Amadora]]
*[[F.C. Paços de Ferreira|Paços de Ferreira]]
*[[Associação Naval 1º de Maio|Naval 1º de Maio]]

The other three teams were replaced by Estrela da Amadora, Paços de Ferreira and Naval 1º de Maio from the Liga de Honra.

===[[Portuguese Footballer of the Year|Footballer of the Year]]===
