The '''2004–05 Primeira Liga''' was the [[Primeira Liga#All-time Primeira Liga table|71st edition]] of top flight of Portuguese football. It started on 28 August 2004 with a match between [[C.F. Os Belenenses|Belenenses]] and [[C.S. Marítimo|Marítimo]], and ended on 22 May 2005.

[[S.L. Benfica|Benfica]] won their 31st league title, with 65 points, three points ahead of the defending champions [[FC Porto|Porto]]. The league was contested by 18 clubs, and was considered one of the most competitive seasons in recent years.

The first goal of the season was scored by Belenenses [[centre-back]] [[Rolando (footballer)|Rolando]]. The first red card of the season was given to [[Vitória F.C.|Vitória de Setúbal]]'s [[Bruno Ribeiro]], and the first yellow was given to Belenenses' [[Juninho Petrolina]] in the opening match of the season. Benfica and Porto were both qualified for the [[2005–06 UEFA Champions League]] group stage, and [[Sporting CP]] qualified for the UEFA Champions League qualifying round. At the bottom of the table, [[Moreirense F.C.|Moreirense]], [[G.D. Estoril-Praia|Estoril]] and [[S.C. Beira-Mar|Beira-Mar]] were relegated to the [[LigaPro|Liga de Honra]]. [[Liédson]] was the top scorer with 25 goals.

==Promotion and relegation==

===Teams relegated to [[LigaPro|Liga de Honra]]===
*[[F.C. Alverca|Alverca]]
*[[F.C. Paços de Ferreira|Paços de Ferreira]]
*[[C.F. Estrela da Amadora|Estrela da Amadora]]

Alverca, Paços de Ferreira, and Estrela da Amadora were consigned to the Liga de Honra following their final classification in [[2003–04 Primeira Liga|2003–04 season]].

===Teams promoted from Liga de Honra===
*[[G.D. Estoril Praia|Estoril]]
*[[Vitória F.C.|Vitória de Setúbal]]
*[[F.C. Penafiel|Penafiel]]

The other three teams were replaced by Estoril, Vitória de Setúbal, and Penafiel from the Liga de Honra.
