The '''2016–17 Third League''' (known as the ''Spor Toto 3. Lig'' for sponsorship reasons) is the 16th season of the league since its establishment in 2001 as the fourth level division; and the 46th season of the third league in [[Turkey|Turkish]] [[Association football|football]] since its establishment in 1967–68.
