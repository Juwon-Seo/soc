The '''2002–03 Primeira Liga''' was the [[Primeira Liga#All-time Primeira Liga table|69th edition]] of top flight of Portuguese football. It started on 25 August 2002 with a match between [[Varzim S.C.|Varzim]] and [[F.C. Paços de Ferreira|Paços de Ferreira]], and ended on 1 June 2003. The league was contested by 18 clubs with [[Sporting CP]] as the defending champions.

[[FC Porto|Porto]] won the league and qualified for the [[2003–04 UEFA Champions League]] group stage, along with [[S.L. Benfica|Benfica]], who qualified for the third round. Sporting and [[U.D. Leiria|União de Leiria]] qualified for the [[2003–04 UEFA Cup]]. In opposite, [[Varzim S.C.|Varzim]], [[C.D. Santa Clara|Santa Clara]] and [[Vitória F.C.|Vitória de Setúbal]] were relegated to the [[LigaPro|Segunda Liga]]. [[Faye Fary]] was the top scorer with 18 goals.

==Promotion and relegation==

===Teams relegated to [[Segunda Liga]]===
*[[S.C. Salgueiros|Salgueiros]]
*[[S.C. Farense|Farense]]
*[[F.C. Alverca|Alverca]]

Salgueiros, Farense and Alverca, were consigned to the Segunda Liga following their final classification in [[2001–02 Primeira Liga|2001–02 season]].

===Teams promoted from Segunda Liga===
*[[Moreirense F.C.|Moreirense]]
*[[Associação Académica de Coimbra – O.A.F.|Académica de Coimbra]]
*[[C.D. Nacional|Nacional]]

The other three teams were replaced by Moreirense, Académica and Nacional from the Segunda Liga.
