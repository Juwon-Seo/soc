The '''2015–16 Süper Lig''', officially called the '''Spor Toto Süper Lig Hasan Doğan season''', was the 58th season of the [[Süper Lig]], the highest tier [[Association football|football]] league of [[Turkey]].

The season was named after [[Hasan Doğan]], a former president of the Turkish Football Federation, who died in 2008.<ref>{{cite web |title=Sözcü |url=https://www.sozcu.com.tr/2015/gunun-icinden/super-lig-2015-2016-sezonu-hasan-dogan-sezonu-oldu-hasan-dogan-kimdir-881559/ |access-date=13 June 2020}}</ref>

== Teams ==
*[[Kayserispor]], [[Osmanlıspor]] and [[Antalyaspor]] promoted from [[2014–15 TFF First League]].
*[[Balıkesirspor]], [[Karabükspor]] and [[Kayseri Erciyesspor]] relegated to [[2015–16 TFF First League]].
