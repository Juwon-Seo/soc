The '''2010–11 [[TFF Second League]]''' (also known as ''[[Spor-Toto]] Second League'' due to sponsorship reasons) is the 10th season of the league since its establishment in 2001 as the third level division; and the 48th season of the second league  in [[Turkey|Turkish]] [[Association football|football]] since its establishment in 1963–64 (before 2001 league was played as second level division).

League was played with 36 teams, 18 in White group and 18 in Red group. Winner of each group promoted to [[2011–12 TFF First League]]. A play off series were played among best four teams in each group to determine the third team to promote. Bottom three teams in each groups  relegated to [[2011–12 TFF Third League]].<ref>See 2010-11 league status on [https://www.tff.org/Resources/TFF/Documents/002011/TFF/Statuler/TFF-2-Lig-Statu-19-08-2010.pdf TFF page]. Retrieved on 06.07.2011. {{in lang|tr}}</ref>

League was started on 29 August 2010 and ended on 8 May 2011. Promotion play-offs were played on 16–22 May 2011.<ref>See start and end dates on [http://www.tff.org/default.aspx?pageID=489 TFF page], [http://www.mackolik.com/National/Default.aspx?id=1708 maçkolik.com] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110628203519/http://www.mackolik.com/National/Default.aspx?id=1708 |date=2011-06-28 }}, or [https://www.angelfire.com/nj/sivritepe/1011/2.html turkish-soccer.com] as alternative sources.</ref>
