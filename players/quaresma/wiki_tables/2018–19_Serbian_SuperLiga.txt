The '''2018–19 Serbian SuperLiga''' was the thirteenth season of the Serbian SuperLiga,   the top tier of [[Association football|football]] league in [[Serbia]]. [[Red Star Belgrade|Red Star]] was the defending champions from the [[2017–18 Serbian SuperLiga|previous season]].

The league consisted of 16 teams: fourteen teams from the [[2017–18 Serbian SuperLiga]] and two new teams from the [[2017–18 Serbian First League]].

'''Teams promoted to the SuperLiga'''

The first club to be promoted was [[FK Proleter Novi Sad|Proleter Novi Sad]], following their 3–1 win against [[FK Radnički 1923|Radnički 1923]] on 20 May 2018. Proleter will play in the [[Serbian SuperLiga]] for the first time in their history. It will also be first time ever that two teams from [[Novi Sad]] are members of top flight.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Proleter piše istoriju |url=http://www.novosti.rs/vesti/sport.294.html:728767-NOVI-SAD-OD-SLEDECE-SEZONE-S-DVA-KLUBA-U-SUPERLIGI-Proleter-pise-istoriju |publisher=novosti.rs |date=22 May 2018 |access-date=22 May 2018}}</ref>

The second club to be promoted was [[FK Dinamo Vranje|Dinamo Vranje]], following their 1–1 draw with [[FK Novi Pazar|Novi Pazar]] on 26 May 2018. Dinamo will play in the [[Serbian SuperLiga]] for the first time in their history.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Dinamo iz Vranja u Superligi |url=https://www.b92.net/sport/fudbal/vesti.php?yyyy=2018&mm=05&dd=26&nav_id=1397364 |publisher=b92.net |date=26 May 2018 |access-date=26 May 2018}}</ref>

'''Teams relegated to the [[Serbian First League|First League]]'''

The first club to be relegated was [[FK Borac Čačak|Borac Čačak]], who were relegated on 5 May 2018 following a 2–1 defeat against [[FK Rad|Rad]], ending their 4-year stay in the top flight.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Rad ispratio Borac iz Superlige |url=http://sportklub.rs/Fudbal/Srbija-Super-liga/a239122-Rad-ispratio-Borac-iz-Superlige.html |publisher=sportklub.rs |date=5 May 2018 |access-date=5 May 2018}}</ref>

The second and final club to be relegated was [[FK Javor Ivanjica|Javor Ivanjica]], who were relegated on 17 May 2018 following their 1–1 draw with [[FK Rad|Rad]], ending their 3-year stay in the top flight.<ref>{{Cite news |title=SUPERLIGAŠKA DRAMA Bačka u 90. minutu opstala u eliti, Javor ispao|url=https://sport.blic.rs/fudbal/domaci-fudbal/superligaska-drama-backa-u-90-minutu-opstala-u-eliti-javor-ispao/39nf9nj |publisher=blic.rs |date=17 May 2018 |access-date=17 May 2018}}</ref>
