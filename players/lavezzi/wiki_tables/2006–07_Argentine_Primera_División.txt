The '''2006–07 Argentina Primera División''' was the 116th season of first division football in Argentina. [[Estudiantes de La Plata|Estudiantes (LP)]] won the Torneo Apertura and [[San Lorenzo de Almagro|San Lorenzo]] won the Torneo Clausura.

[[Godoy Cruz Antonio Tomba|Godoy Cruz]], [[Club Atlético Nueva Chicago|Nueva Chicago]], and [[CA Belgrano|Belgrano]] were promoted from the [[2005–06 Primera B Nacional]].

At the end of the season, Belgrano and [[Quilmes Atlético Club|Quilmes]] were directly relegated to the [[Primera B Nacional]] and [[Club Atlético Huracán|Huracán]] and [[Club Atlético Tigre|Tigre]] promoted from Primera B Nacional after winning the relegation playoff against Godoy Cruz and Nueva Chicago respectively. Therefore, a total of four teams were relegated to the second division.

===Championship playoff===
{{main|2006 Torneo Apertura Final}}
[[Boca Juniors]] and [[Estudiantes de La Plata|Estudiantes LP]] ended up tied in points at the end of the 19 weeks of regular season. Tournament rules establish that, unlike any other position on the table, if two or more teams are equal in points at the end of play, goal difference does not count and a playoff game is required. Estudiantes won that match and was crowned as champion.
