The '''2005–06 [[Argentine Primera División]]''' was the 115th season of top-flight [[football in Argentina]]. The season ran from 5 August 2005 to 14 May 2006.<ref name=rsssf1ra>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesa/arg06.html Argentina 2005–06] by Javier Roimiser on RSSSF.com</ref> 

[[Tiro Federal]] ([[2004–05 Primera B Nacional]] champion)<ref>[https://www.lacapital.com.ar/ovacion/a-15-anos-del-inmenso-tiro-federal-n2593082.html A 15 años del inmenso Tiro Federal]</ref> and [[Gimnasia y Esgrima de Jujuy]] (after beating [[Club Atlético Huracán|Huracán]] in a second promotion playoff) were promoted from [[Primera B Nacional]].<ref>[https://www.jujuyalmomento.com/ascenso/a-15-anos-del-segundo-ascenso-primera-division-gimnasia-y-esgrima-jujuy-n108703 A 15 años del segundo ascenso a primera división de Gimnasia y Esgrima de Jujuy] on Jujyy al Momento, 2 Jul 2020</ref><ref>[https://www.infobae.com/2005/07/02/193984-gimnasia-jujuy-ascendio-primera-division/ Gimnasia de Jujuy ascendió a Primera], Infobae, 2 Jul 2005</ref><ref name=rsssf2da>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesa/arg2-05.html Argentina Second Level (Primera B - Nacional) 2004/05] by Gurgen Mahari on the RSSSF</ref>

[[Boca Juniors]] won both, Apertura and Clausura championships totalising 28 league titles to date,<ref>[https://www.historiadeboca.com.ar/torneo-apertura-2005/2005/41.html Torneo Apertura 2005 - Campaña] on Historia de Boca</ref><ref>[https://www.ole.com.ar/boca-juniors/14-anos-bicampeonato-boca-2006-basile_0_Vd8IrKKfq.html A 14 años del bicampeonato del Coco] on Olé, 7 May 2020</ref> while [[Instituto de Córdoba|Instituto (C)]], [[Tiro Federal]] and [[Club Olimpo|Olimpo]] were relegated.<ref name=rsssf1ra/>
