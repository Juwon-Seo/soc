The '''2004–05 [[Terceira Divisão]]''' season was the 58th season of the competition and the 15th season of recognised fourth-tier [[Association football|football]] in Portugal.<ref name="Soccer Library">{{cite web|url=http://soccerlibrary.free.fr/por_ft.pdf|title=Portugal – Table of Honor – soccerlibrary.free.fr|format=|work=Soccer Library|accessdate=2012-05-15|archive-date=2019-10-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191010211315/http://soccerlibrary.free.fr/por_ft.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Overview==
The league was contested by 118 teams in 7 divisions of 10 to 18 teams.<ref name="Soccer Library"/>
