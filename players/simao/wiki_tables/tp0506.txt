The '''2005–06 [[Taça de Portugal]]''' was the 66th edition of the Portuguese [[Association football|football]] knockout tournament, organized by the [[Portuguese Football Federation]] (FPF). The 2005–06 Taça de Portugal began on 4 September 2005.{{citation needed|date=June 2026}} The final was played on 14 May 2006 at the [[Estádio Nacional]].

[[Vitória F.C.|Vitória de Setúbal]] were the previous holders, having defeated [[S.L. Benfica|Benfica]] 2–1, in the previous season's final.{{citation needed|date=May 2026}} However, Vitória de Setúbal was not able to regain their title as they were defeated in the final by Porto. Porto defeated Vitória de Setúbal, 1–0 to win their fourth title in seven seasons and claim their 13th Taça de Portugal in their history.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.foradejogo.net/match.php?match=2006154500101020| title=Taça de Portugal - Final|publisher=ForaDeJogo|language= pt |access-date=1 January 2013}}</ref>{{citation needed|date=June 2026}}

Since Porto had already qualified for the [[2006–07 UEFA Champions League]] as domestic title holders,{{citation needed|date=June 2026}} Vitória by reaching the final of the Taça de Portugal, had guaranteed a place in the [[2006–07 UEFA Cup#First round|2006–07 UEFA Cup]]. As Porto won both the league and cup in the same season, Vitória would qualify for the [[2006 Supertaça Cândido de Oliveira]] as the cup runner-up.{{citation needed|date=June 2026}}

==First round==
For the first round draw, teams were drawn against each other in accordance to their geographical location. The draw was split up into four sections: teams from the north, the center, the south and the [[Azores]] region.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.record.xl.pt/arquivo/interior.aspx?content_id=247384|title=1.ª eliminatória|trans-title=1.ª round|publisher=[[Record (Portuguese newspaper)|Record]]|language= pt |date=4 September 2005|access-date=19 January 2013}}</ref> Ties were played on 4 September.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.foradejogo.net/tournament.php?round=2006154500199|title=Taça de Portugal 2005/2006 1ª Eliminatória|trans-title=Cup of Portugal 2005/2006 1ª Round|publisher=ForaDeJogo|language=pt|access-date=19 January 2013}}</ref>{{citation needed|date=June 2026}} In the first round, three sides forfeited their respective matches thus allowing their opponents to proceed to the next round. The first round of the cup saw teams from the [[Terceira Divisão]] (IV) start the competition alongside some teams who registered to participate in the cup from the [[Portuguese District Football Associations|Portuguese District Leagues]] (V).

==Fourth round==
The draw for the fourth round took place on 10 October.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.record.xl.pt/arquivo/interior.aspx?content_id=250201| title=Sorteio da 4ª eliminatória|trans-title=Draw for 4ª round|publisher=[[Record (Portuguese newspaper)|Record]]|language= pt |date=10 October 2005|access-date=31 December 2012}}</ref>  The majority of games were played on 26 October. [[U.S.C. Paredes|Parades]]'s cup tie against [[S.L. Nelas|Nelas]] was played on 30 October whilst the ties involving [[F.C. Paços de Ferreira|Paços de Ferreira]] and [[G.D. Tourizense|Tourizense]], and [[Associação Naval 1º de Maio|Naval]] and [[A.D. Pontassolense|Pontassolense]] were played on 12–13 November.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.foradejogo.net/tournament.php?round=2006154500196 | title= Taça de Portugal 2005/2006 4ª Eliminatória|trans-title=Cup of Portugal 2005/2006 4ª Round|publisher=ForaDeJogo|language= pt |access-date=27 December 2012}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/edicao.php?id_edicao=1147&fase=10934| title=Taça de Portugal 2005/2006 4ª ELIMINATÓRIA|trans-title=Cup of Portugal 2005/2006 4ª Round|publisher=ZeroZero|language= pt |access-date=1 January 2013}}</ref> Due to the odd number of games in the third round, [[Vitória S.C.|Vitória de Guimarães]] progressed to the next round due to having no opponent to face at this stage of the competition.
