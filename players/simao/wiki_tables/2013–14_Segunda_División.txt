The '''2013–14 [[Segunda División]]''' season (known as the ''Liga Adelante'' for sponsorship reasons<ref>{{cite news |title=BBVA seguirá patrocinando la Liga por 23,5 millones al año |trans-title=BBVA will continue sponsoring La Liga for €23,5M per year |url=http://cincodias.com/cincodias/2013/06/11/empresas/1370968618_930668.html |publisher=[[:es:Cinco Días|Cinco Días]] |date=11 June 2013 |access-date=1 July 2013}}</ref>) was the 83rd since its establishment. The season started on 17 August 2013 and the league phase of 42 rounds ended on 8 June 2014. The entire season ended on 22 June 2014 with the promotion play-off finals.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.lfp.es/Noticias2/DetalleNoticia2/tabid/444/Article/4688/language/es-ES/proyecto-de-calendario-para-la-temporada-201314.aspx |title=Proyecto de calendario para la temporada 2013/14 |trans-title=Calendar proposal for 2013–14 season |language=es |publisher=[[Liga de Fútbol Profesional|LFP]] |date=6 June 2013 |access-date=9 June 2013 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130609041425/http://www.lfp.es/Noticias2/DetalleNoticia2/tabid/444/Article/4688/language/es-ES/proyecto-de-calendario-para-la-temporada-201314.aspx |archive-date=9 June 2013 }}</ref>

== Teams ==

===Promotion and relegation (pre-season)===
A total of 22 teams contested the league, including 15 sides from the [[2012–13 Segunda División|2012–13 season]], four promoted from the [[2012–13 Segunda División B]] and three relegated from the [[2012–13 La Liga]].

[[RCD Mallorca]], [[Deportivo de La Coruña]] and [[Real Zaragoza]] were the teams relegated from [[La Liga]] the previous season. Mallorca was relegated after sixteen years in [[La Liga]], the longest period in its history and ending their golden era, Zaragoza returned to the Segunda División after a four-year tenure in La Liga, while Deportivo de La Coruña made an immediate return to the Segunda División after being promoted the previous year. All three teams were relegated in the last matchday.<ref>{{cite news |title=Mallorca, Deportivo de La Coruña y Zaragoza, pierden la categoría y descienden de la Liga BBVA |trans-title=Mallorca, Deportivo de La Coruña and Zaragoza lose their status and are relegated from Liga BBVA |url=http://www.lfp.es/Noticias2/DetalleNoticia2/tabid/444/article/4592/language/es-ES/Default.aspx |language=es |publisher=[[Liga de Fútbol Profesional|LFP]] |date=2 June 2013 |access-date=9 June 2013}}</ref> [[Elche CF]] was promoted [[2012–13 Segunda División|the previous season]] to La Liga after 14 consecutive seasons in the Segunda División.<ref>{{cite news |title=Un líder absoluto y austero |trans-title=An absolute and clear leader |url=http://deportes.elpais.com/deportes/2013/05/18/actualidad/1368902119_242372.html |language=es |work=[[El País]] |date=18 May 2013 |access-date=9 June 2013}}</ref> [[Villarreal CF|Villarreal]] made an immediate return to La Liga<ref>{{cite news |title=El Villarreal es de Primera |trans-title=Villarreal is in the top flight |url=http://futbol.as.com/futbol/2013/06/08/mas_futbol/1370706510_996039.html |language=es |work=[[Diario AS|As]] |date=8 June 2013 |access-date=9 June 2013}}</ref> after a win over Almería in the decisive match of the last matchday where the winner would be directly promoted to La Liga.<ref>{{cite news |title=La gran final por el ascenso |trans-title=The grand final for promotion |url=http://www.mundodeportivo.com/20130608/futbol/liga-segunda-division/ultima-jornada-liga-adelante-villarreal-almeria_54375457942.html |language=es |work=[[El Mundo Deportivo]] |date=8 June 2013 |access-date=9 June 2013}}</ref> [[UD Almería|Almería]] was promoted on 22 June 2013 as winner of [[2013 Segunda División play-offs|play-off games]] after two years of absence in La Liga.<ref>{{cite The teams which had been relegated from the Segunda División the [[2012–13 Segunda División|previous season]] were [[CD Guadalajara (Spain)|Guadalajara]], [[Racing de Santander|Racing Santander]], [[SD Huesca|Huesca]] and [[Xerez CD|Xerez]]. Xerez was relegated after twelve years in the division some weeks before the end of season.<ref>{{cite news |title=El Xerez desciende a 2ª B en ocho minutos |trans-title=Xerez relegated to 2ª B in matchday.<ref>{{cite news |title=Desciende al Guadalajara y Huesca, Murcia y Racing respiran |trans-title=Guadalajara relegated and Huesca, Murcia and Racing breathe again after five years in the second level, while Racing de Santander returned to the third level after twenty years and nineteen of them in La Liga.<ref>{{cite news |title=El Racing consuma su debacle |trans-title=Racing finishes its debacle |url=http://www.marca.com/2013/06/08/futbol/2adivision/1370725059.html |language=es |work=[[Marca (newspaper)|Marca]] |date=8 June 2013 |access-date=10 June }}</ref>

The four teams relegated were replaced by four Segunda División B teams: [[Deportivo Alavés]] ([[2012–13 Segunda División B#League table 2|group 2 champions]] and [[2013 Segunda División B play-offs#Final|overall champions]]) and [[CD Tenerife]] ([[2012–13 Segunda División B#League table|group 1 champions]] and [[2013 Segunda División B play-offs#Final|overall runners-up]]), both from the [[2013 Segunda División B play-offs#Group Winners promotion play-off|champions]] play-offs and two from the [[2013 Segunda División B play-offs#Third round|third round play-offs]]: [[SD Eibar|Eibar]] and [[Real Jaén|Jaén]]. Alavés and Tenerife returned to the second level respectively after 4 and 2 years,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.estadiodeportivo.com/futbol-segunda-division/2013/06/02/alaves-tenerife-ascienden-segunda-division/5422.html |title=Alavés y Tenerife ascienden a Segunda división |trans-title=Alavés and Tenerife promote to Segunda División |language=es |publisher=Estadio Deportivo |date=2 June 2013 |access-date=9 June 2013}}</ref> while Eibar and Jaén returned to the second level respectively after 4 and 11 years.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.marca.com/2013/06/30/futbol/mas_futbol/1372621872.html |title=El Eibar vuelve a Segunda división cuatro años después |trans-title=Eibar returns to Segunda división four years later |language=es |work=[[Marca (newspaper)|Marca]] |date=30 June 2013 |access-date=30 June 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.marca.com/2013/06/30/futbol/mas_futbol/1372622338.html |title=El Real Jaén, ascenso sufrido |trans-title=Real Jaén, promotion suffered |language=es |work=[[Marca (newspaper)|Marca]] |date=30 June 2013 |access-date=30 June 2013}}</ref>

====Guadalajara case====
Guadalajara was relegated to Segunda División B administratively after irregularities detected in the capital increase made the previous summer for conversion to a [[Sociedad Anónima Deportiva]].<ref>{{cite news |title=El Guadalajara, descendido en los despachos a Segunda B |trans-title=Guadalajara, relegated to Segunda B |url=http://www.lavozdegalicia.es/noticia/deportes/2013/06/04/guadalajara-descendido-despachos-segunda-b/00031370354441262569300.htm |language=es |publisher=[[La Voz de Galicia]] |date=4 June 2013 |access-date=9 June 2013}}</ref> Murcia remained in Segunda after the relegation administratively of Guadalajara, however [[Royal Spanish Football Federation|RFEF]] didn't recognize the unilateral sanction by [[Liga de Fútbol Profesional|LFP]]<ref>{{cite news |title=El descenso del Guadalajara enfrenta a la Liga y a la Federación |trans-title=The relegation of Guadalajara confronts La Liga and Federación |url=http://www.elconfidencial.com/deportes/futbol/2013/06/15/el-descenso-del-guadalajara-enfrenta-a-la-liga-y-a-la-federacion-123077/ |language=es |publisher=[[:es:El Confidencial|El Confidencial]] |date=15 June 2013 |access-date=17 July 2013 |archive-date=21 July 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130721105802/http://www.elconfidencial.com/deportes/futbol/2013/06/15/el-descenso-del-guadalajara-enfrenta-a-la-liga-y-a-la-federacion-123077/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> and the relegated or saved team was disputed between Guadalajara and Murcia until Spanish sports jurisdictional body, the [[Spanish Sports Disciplinary Committee|CEDD]] (part of [[Consejo Superior de Deportes|CSD]]), would make a resolution.<ref>{{cite news |title=Equipo X en vez de Real Murcia y Guadalajara en el bombo |trans-title=Team X and not Real Murcia neither Guadalajara in the hype |url=http://futbol.as.com/futbol/2013/07/09/primera/1373336331_577151.html |language=es |work=[[Diario AS|As]] |date=9 July 2013 |access-date=17 July 2013}}</ref> CEDD ruled refusing the precautionary suspension of Guadalajara relegation, whereby Murcia remained in Segunda.<ref>{{cite news |title=El CEDD deniega la suspensión cautelar del descenso al Guadalajara |trans-title=CEDD denies precautionary suspension of the Guadalajara relegation |url=http://www.marca.com/2013/07/12/futbol/equipos/guadalajara/1373651418.html |language=es |work=[[Marca ==Promotion play-offs==
{{main|2014 Segunda División play-offs}}
This promotion phase (known as ''Promoción de ascenso'') is to determine the third team which will be promoted to [[2014–15 La Liga]]. Teams placed between 3rd and 6th position (excluding reserve teams) take part in the promotion play-offs. Fifth placed faces against the fourth, while the sixth positioned team faces against the third. The first leg of the semi-finals will be played on 11 June, the best positioned team plays at home the second leg on 15 June. The final will also be two-legged, with the first leg on 19 June and the second leg on 22 June, with the best positioned team also playing at home the second leg.

In case of a draw after the overtime in the second leg, there will not be a penalty shoot-out, and the best positioned team in the league table directly passes to the next round.
