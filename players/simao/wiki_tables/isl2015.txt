The '''2015 Hero Indian Super League''' was the second season of the [[Indian Super League]], a professional [[association football|football]] league played in [[India]] since 2014. The season featured eight teams. The regular season kicked off on 3 October and ended on 6 December, while the finals began on 11 December, which will conclude with the final match on 20 December.<ref>{{cite web|title=Indian Super League Schedule|url=http://www.indiansuperleague.com/isl-2015/schedules-fixtures|website=Indian Super League|access-date=2015-06-05|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150912044453/http://www.indiansuperleague.com/isl-2015/schedules-fixtures|archive-date=2015-09-12|url-status=dead}}</ref> The defending champions [[Atlético de Kolkata]] were eliminated in the semi-finals by [[Chennaiyin FC]]. The [[2015 Indian Super League final|final]] was played between [[FC Goa|Goa]] and Chennaiyin on 20 December 2015 at the [[Fatorda Stadium]] in [[Goa]]. Chennayin were crowned as champions defeating Goa 3–2 in the final.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.firstpost.com/sports/isl-final-chennaiyin-fc-snatch-victory-from-the-jaws-of-defeat-to-beat-goa-3-2-in-thriller-2553270.html|title=ISL Final: Chennaiyin FC snatch victory from the jaws of defeat to beat Goa 3-2 in thriller|publisher=Firstpost|date=20 December 2015|access-date=20 December 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151221035105/http://www.firstpost.com/sports/isl-final-chennaiyin-fc-snatch-victory-from-the-jaws-of-defeat-to-beat-goa-3-2-in-thriller-2553270.html|archive-date=21 December 2015|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.indiansuperleague.com/news/3495-chennai-beat-goa-in-a-3-2-thriller-to-become-hero-isl-2015-champions|title=Chennai beat Goa in a 3-2 thriller to become Hero ISL 2015 champions|publisher=ISL|date=20 December 2015|access-date=20 December 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151222085827/http://www.indiansuperleague.com/news/3495-chennai-beat-goa-in-a-3-2-thriller-to-become-hero-isl-2015-champions|archive-date=22 December 2015|url-status=live}}</ref>
