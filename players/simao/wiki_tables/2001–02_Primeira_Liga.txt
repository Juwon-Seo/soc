The '''2001–02 Primeira Liga''' was the [[Primeira Liga#All-time Primeira Liga table|68th edition]] of top flight of Portuguese football. It started on 11 August 2001 with a match between [[Varzim S.C.|Varzim]] and [[S.L. Benfica|Benfica]], and ended on 6 May 2002. The league was contested by 18 clubs with [[Boavista F.C.|Boavista]] as the defending champions.

[[Sporting CP]] won the league and qualified for the [[2002–03 UEFA Champions League]] third qualifying round, along with Boavista, who qualified for the second round. [[FC Porto|Porto]], Benfica and [[Leixões S.C.|Leixões]] from the [[2001–02 Segunda Divisão B|third division]] qualified for the [[2002–03 UEFA Cup]], while [[S.C. Salgueiros|Salgueiros]], [[S.C. Farense|Farense]] and [[F.C. Alverca|Alverca]] were relegated to the [[LigaPro|Segunda Liga]]. [[Mário Jardel]] was the top scorer with 42 goals. Until 2021, this remained the last season where [[Sporting CP]] won the title and where neither [[FC Porto|Porto]] nor [[S.L. Benfica|Benfica]] won before Sporting CP won the 2020–21 season ending their 19-year drought and the 19-year reign of Porto and Benfica.

==Promotion and relegation==

===Teams relegated to [[Segunda Liga]]===
*[[S.C. Campomaiorense|Campomaiorense]]
*[[C.D. Aves|Desportivo das Aves]]
*[[C.F. Estrela da Amadora|Estrela da Amadora]]

Campomaiorense, Desportivo das Aves and Estrela da Amadora were consigned to the Segunda Liga following their final classification in the [[2000–01 Primeira Liga|2000–01 season]].

===Teams promoted from Segunda Liga===
*[[C.D. Santa Clara|Santa Clara]]
*[[Varzim S.C.|Varzim]]
*[[Vitória F.C.|Vitória de Setúbal]]

The other three teams were replaced by Santa Clara, Varzim and Vitória de Setúbal from the Segunda Liga.
