The 2006–07 '''[[Taça de Portugal]]''' was the 67th edition of the Portuguese [[Association football|football]] knockout tournament, organized by the [[Portuguese Football Federation]] (FPF). The 2006–07 Taça de Portugal began on 3 September 2006.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/edicao.php?id_edicao=1317&fase=10940| title=Taça de Portugal 2006/2007 1ª ELIMINATÓRIA|trans-title=Cup of Portugal 2006/2007 1ª Round|publisher=ZeroZero|language= pt |access-date=13 January 2013}}</ref> The final was played on 27 May 2007 at the [[Estádio Nacional]].

[[FC Porto|Porto]] were the previous holders, having defeated [[Vitória F.C.|Vitória de Setúbal]] 1–0  in the previous season's final.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/jogo.php?id=221493| title=FC Porto 1-0 V. Setúbal|publisher=ZeroZero|language= pt |access-date=13 January 2013}}</ref> Porto was not able to regain their title as they were defeated by [[Atlético Clube de Portugal|Atlético CP]] in the fourth round.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/jogo.php?id=244118| title=FC Porto 0-1 Atlético CP|publisher=ZeroZero|language= pt |access-date=13 January 2013}}</ref> [[Sporting CP]] defeated [[C.F. Os Belenenses|Belenenses]] 1–0 in the final to win their 14th Taça de Portugal.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/jogo.php?id=253611| title=Belenenses 0-1 Sporting|publisher=ZeroZero|language= pt |access-date=13 January 2013}}</ref> By winning the Taça de Portugal, Sporting CP qualified for the [[2007 Supertaça Cândido de Oliveira]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/jogo.php?id=261890| title=FC Porto 0-1 Sporting|publisher=ZeroZero|language= pt |access-date=13 January 2013}}</ref>

==First round==
For the first round draw, teams were drawn against each other in accordance to their geographical location. The draw was split up into four sections: teams from the north, the center, the south and the [[Azores]] region. The draw for the first round took place on 8 August.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.record.xl.pt/arquivo/interior.aspx?content_id=279451 |title=Sorteio da 1.ª eliminatória |trans-title=Draw for the 1.ª round |publisher=[[Record (Portuguese newspaper)|Record]] |language=pt |date=8 August 2006 |access-date=23 June 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303195932/http://www.record.xl.pt/arquivo/interior.aspx?content_id=279451 |archive-date=3 March 2016 |url-status=live }}</ref> All first round cup ties were played on 3 September.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.foradejogo.net/tournament.php?round=2007154500199 |title=Taça de Portugal 2006/2007 1ª Eliminatória |trans-title=Cup of Portugal 2006/2007 1ª Round |publisher=ForaDeJogo |language=pt |access-date=23 June 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140306165752/http://www.foradejogo.net/tournament.php?round=2007154500199 |archive-date=6 March 2014 |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/edicao.php?id_edicao=1317&fase=10940|title=Taça de Portugal 2006/2007 1ª ELIMINATÓRIA|trans-title=Cup of Portugal 2006/2007 1ª Round|publisher=ZeroZero|language=pt|access-date=23 June 2013}}</ref> Due to the odd number of teams involved at this stage of the competition, [[S.U. 1º de Dezembro|1º de Dezembro]], [[Canedo F.C.|Canedo]], Cartaxo, Lajense, [[CA Macedo de Cavaleiros|Macedo de Cavaleiros]], [[Penamacorense]] and [[U.D. Serra|União da Serra]] progressed to the next round.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.record.xl.pt/arquivo/interior.aspx?content_id=281830 |title=Resultados (1.ª eliminatória) |trans-title=Results (1.ª round) |publisher=[[Record (Portuguese newspaper)|Record]] |language=pt |date=3 September 2006 |access-date=24 June 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303195932/http://www.record.xl.pt/arquivo/interior.aspx?content_id=281830 |archive-date=3 March 2016 |url-status=live }}</ref> Vasco da Gama AC were scheduled to play [[Casa Pia A.C.|Casa Pia]], but forfeited their match due to the club folding at the beginning of the 2006–07 season.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/jogo.php?id=229966|title=Casa Pia FC Vasco Gama|publisher=ZeroZero|language=pt|access-date=25 June 2013}}</ref> The first round of the cup saw teams from the [[Terceira Divisão]] (IV) start the competition alongside some teams who registered to participate in the cup from the [[Portuguese District Football Associations|Portuguese District Leagues]] (V).

==Fourth round==
The draw for the fourth round took place on 28 November.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.record.xl.pt/arquivo/interior.aspx?content_id=289876|title=Grandes com caminho facilitado|trans-title=Big teams with easier draw|publisher=[[Record (Portuguese newspaper)|Record]]|language= pt|date=28 November 2006|access-date=22 June 2013}}</ref> [[Sporting CP]]'s cup tie against [[C.F. União|União da Madeira]] was played on 21 December, whilst [[S.L. Benfica|Benfica]]'s tie against [[Oliveira do Bairro S.C.|Oliveira do Bairro]] was played on 6 January.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.foradejogo.net/match.php?match=2007154500196010|title=União Madeira 1 - 3 Sporting|publisher=ForaDeJogo|language=pt|access-date=22 June 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.foradejogo.net/match.php?match=2007154500196020|title=Benfica 5 - 0 Oliveira do Bairro|publisher=ForaDeJogo|language=pt|access-date=22 June 2013}}</ref> The remainder of the ties were played on 7 January.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.foradejogo.net/tournament.php?round=2007154500196|title=Taça de Portugal 2006/2007 4ª Eliminatória|trans-title=Cup of Portugal 2006/2007 4ª Round|publisher=ForaDeJogo|language=pt|access-date=22 June 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/edicao.php?id_edicao=1317&fase=10943|title=Taça de Portugal 2006/2007 4ª ELIMINATÓRIA|trans-title=Cup of Portugal 2006/2007 4ª Round|publisher=ZeroZero|language= pt|access-date=22 June 2013}}</ref> Due to the odd number of teams in the competition, [[Rio Ave F.C.|Rio Ave]] progressed to the next round due to having no opponent to face at this stage of the competition. The fourth round saw teams from the [[Primeira Liga]] (I) enter the competition.
