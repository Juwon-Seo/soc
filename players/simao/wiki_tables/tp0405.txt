The 2004–05 '''[[Taça de Portugal]]''' was the 65th edition of the Portuguese [[Association football|football]] knockout tournament, organized by the [[Portuguese Football Federation]] (FPF). The 2004–05 Taça de Portugal began on 5 September 2004.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/edicao.php?id_edicao=549&fase=10805|title=Taça de Portugal 2004/2005 1ª ELIMINATÓRIA|trans-title=Cup of Portugal 2004/2005 1ª Round|publisher=ZeroZero|language= pt|access-date=25 June 2013}}</ref> The final was played on 29 May 2005 at the [[Estádio Nacional]].

[[S.L. Benfica|Benfica]] were the previous holders, having defeated [[F.C. Porto|Porto]] 2–1  in the previous season's final.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/jogo.php?id=47041|title=FC Porto 1-2 (a.p.) Benfica|trans-title=FC Porto 1-2 (a.e.t.) Benfica|publisher=ZeroZero|language= pt|access-date=25 June 2013}}</ref> Vitória de Setúbal defeated holders Benfica, 2–1 in the final to win their third Taça de Portugal.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/jogo.php?id=183253| title=Benfica 1-2 V. Setúbal|publisher=ZeroZero|language= pt|access-date=25 June 2013}}</ref> 
By virtue of their victory in the final, Vitória qualified for the [[2005–06 UEFA Cup first round]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/edicao.php?op=&id_edicao=1117|title=Taça UEFA 2005/06|trans-title=UEFA Cup 2005/06|publisher=ZeroZero|language=pt|access-date=25 June 2013}}</ref> They would also qualify for the [[2005 Supertaça Cândido de Oliveira]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/jogo.php?id=186529|title=Benfica 1-0 V. Setúbal|publisher=ZeroZero|language=pt|access-date=25 June 2013}}</ref>

==First round==
For the first round draw, teams were drawn against each other in accordance to their geographical location. The draw was split up into four sections: teams from the north, the center, the south and the [[Azores]] region.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablesp/portcup05.html|title=Portugal Cup 2004/05|publisher=[[Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation|RSSSF]]|access-date=3 July 2013}}</ref> All first round cup ties were played on the 5–15 September.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.foradejogo.net/tournament.php?round=2005154500199 |title=Taça de Portugal 2004/2005 1ª Eliminatória |trans-title=Cup of Portugal 2004/2005 1ª Round |publisher=ForaDeJogo |language=pt |access-date=23 June 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160305011600/http://www.foradejogo.net/tournament.php?round=2005154500199 |archive-date=5 March 2016 |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.zerozero.pt/edicao.php?id_edicao=549&fase=10805|title=Taça de Portugal 2004/2005 1ª ELIMINATÓRIA|trans-title=Cup of Portugal 2004/2005 1ª Round|publisher=ZeroZero|language=pt|access-date=29 June 2013}}</ref> Due to the odd number of teams at this stage of the competition, [[S.C. Angrense|Angrense]] progressed to the next round due to having no opponent to face at this stage of the competition. The first round of the cup saw teams from the [[Terceira Divisão]] (IV) start the competition alongside some teams who registered to participate in the cup from the [[Portuguese District Football Associations|Portuguese District Leagues]] (V).
