The '''2011–12 [[Ukrainian Premier League]]''' season was the 21st since its establishment and fourth since its reorganisation. The season began on 8 July 2011 when newly promoted [[PFC Oleksandria]] visited [[FC Vorskla Poltava]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ua-football.com/ukrainian/high/4e0c5c91.html|title=Дати та час початку матчів 1-го туру ПЛ (Date and time of match in the First Round)|publisher=ua-football.com|work=[[Ukraine Premier League]]|access-date=30 June 2011|date=30 June 2011|language=uk}}</ref> [[FC Shakhtar Donetsk]] were the defending champions, having won their 6th league title in the [[2010–11 Ukrainian Premier League|2010–11 season]] and they successfully defended their title by winning the championship in the last round of the competition.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fifa.com/worldfootball/clubfootball/news/newsid=1629029.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120514024153/http://www.fifa.com/worldfootball/clubfootball/news/newsid=1629029.html|url-status=dead|archive-date=14 May 2012|title=Shakhtar win seventh Ukrainian title|date= 10 May 2012|work=FIFA.com|access-date=29 May 2012 }}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.irishexaminer.com/sport/columnists/jonathan-wilson/shakhtar-donetsks-unlikely-hero-193774.html|title=Donetsk’s unlikely hero|date= 14 May 2012|work=Irish Examiner|access-date=29 May 2012 }}</ref>

A total of sixteen teams participated in the league, the best fourteen sides of the 2010–11 season and two promoted clubs from the [[2010–11 Ukrainian First League]].

The competition had a winter break which began on 11 December 2011 and the season resumed on 3 March 2012. The season concluded on 10 May 2012.<ref name="rsssf"/>
