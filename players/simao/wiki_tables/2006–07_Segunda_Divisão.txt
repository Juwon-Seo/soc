The '''2006–07 [[Segunda Divisão]]''' season was the 73rd season of the competition and the 60th season of recognised third-tier [[Association football|football]] in Portugal.<ref name="Soccer Library">{{cite web|url=http://soccerlibrary.free.fr/por_ft.pdf|title=Portugal – Table of Honor – soccerlibrary.free.fr|work=Soccer Library|access-date=2012-05-16|archive-date=2019-10-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191010211315/http://soccerlibrary.free.fr/por_ft.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Overview==
The league was contested by 56 teams in 4 divisions with [[SC Freamunde]], [[União Funchal]], [[CD Fátima]] and [[Real S.C.|Real]] winning the respective divisional competitions and progressing to the championship playoffs.  The overall championship was won by [[SC Freamunde]] and the runners-up [[CD Fátima]] were also promoted to the [[Liga de Honra]].<ref name="Soccer Library"/>
