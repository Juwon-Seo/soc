The '''2001–02''' season of the [[Belgian First Division A|Jupiler League]] began on August 11, 2001, and ended on May 5, 2002.  [[K.R.C. Genk|Racing Genk]] became champions.

These teams were promoted from the [[Belgian Second Division|second division]] at the start of the season:
*[[K.F.C. Lommel S.K.|Lommel]] (second division champion)
*[[R.W.D. Molenbeek (1909)|R.W.D. Molenbeek]] (playoff winner)

==Relegated teams==
These teams were relegated to the second division at the end of the season:
*[[SC Eendracht Aalst|Eendracht Aalst]] (no license)
*[[R.W.D. Molenbeek (1909)|R.W.D. Molenbeek]] (no license)
