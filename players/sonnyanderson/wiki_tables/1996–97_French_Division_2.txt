The '''1996–97 Division 2 season''', organised by the [[Ligue de Football Professionnel|LNF]] was won by [[LB Châteauroux]] and saw the promotions of [[LB Châteauroux]] and [[Toulouse FC]], whereas [[FC Perpignan]], [[FCO Charleville]], [[SAS Épinal]] and [[Stade Briochin]] were relegated to [[Championnat National|National]].
