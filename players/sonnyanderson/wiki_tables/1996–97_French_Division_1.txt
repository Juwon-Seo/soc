The '''1996–97 Division 1 season''' was the 59th since its establishment. [[AS Monaco FC|AS Monaco]] won the [[Ligue de Football Professionnel|French Association Football League]] with 79 points. Four teams were relegated to [[Ligue 2|Second division]] and only two were promoted because in 1997–1998, only 18 would participate the championship.
