The '''Division 2 season 2000/2001''', organised by the [[Ligue de Football Professionnel|LNF]] was won by [[FC Sochaux-Montbéliard]] and saw the [[promotion and relegation|promotions]] of [[FC Sochaux-Montbéliard]], [[FC Lorient]] and [[Montpellier HSC]], whereas [[AS Cannes]] and [[Angers SCO]] were [[promotion and relegation|relegated]] to [[Championnat National|National]].

==Recap==
* '''Promoted to L1''' : [[FC Sochaux-Montbéliard]], [[FC Lorient]], [[Montpellier HSC]] 
* '''Relegated to L2''' : [[RC Strasbourg]], [[AS Saint-Étienne]]
* '''Promoted to L2''' : [[Grenoble Foot 38]], [[Amiens SC]], [[FC Istres]]
* '''Relegated to National''' : [[AS Cannes]], [[Angers SCO]] ([[FC Martigues]] were not relegated since [[Toulouse FC]] were relegated from [[Ligue 1|Division 1]] to [[Championnat National|National]])
