The '''1993–94 Division 1 season''' was the 56th since its establishment. [[Paris Saint-Germain F.C.|Paris Saint-Germain]] became champions for the second time in their history with 59 points.

This was the final season in which two points were awarded for a win; going forward this changed to [[Three points for a win|three points]].
