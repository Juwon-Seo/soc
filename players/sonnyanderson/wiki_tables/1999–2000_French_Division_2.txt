The '''Division 2 season 1999/2000''', organised by the [[Ligue de Football Professionnel|LNF]] was won by [[Lille OSC]] and saw the promotions of [[Lille OSC]], [[EA Guingamp]] and [[Toulouse FC]], whereas [[Amiens SC]], [[ASOA Valence]] and [[CS Louhans-Cuiseaux]] were relegated to [[Championnat National|National]].
