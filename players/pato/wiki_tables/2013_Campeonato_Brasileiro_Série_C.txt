The 2013 '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C]]''', the third level of the Brazilian League, was contested by 21 clubs, and started on June 1 and ended on December 1, 2013. The four teams in the semifinals were promoted to the [[2014 Campeonato Brasileiro Série B]],<ref name=uol_esporte_rules >{{cite web |url=http://esporte.uol.com.br/futebol/campeonatos/brasileiro/2013/serie-c/regulamento/ |title=Regulamento Campeonato Brasileiro Série C 2013 |access-date=September 10, 2013 |publisher=UOL Esporte |language=pt }}</ref> and the worst three teams in Group A and the two worst teams in Group B were relegated to the [[2014 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://imagens.cbf.com.br/201306/2096258152.PDF |title=Diretrizes Técnicas |access-date=September 10, 2013 |publisher=CBF |language=pt |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131204044217/http://imagens.cbf.com.br/201306/2096258152.PDF |archive-date=2013-12-04 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
