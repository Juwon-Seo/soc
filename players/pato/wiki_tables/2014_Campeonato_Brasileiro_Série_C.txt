The '''[[Campeonato Brasileiro Série C|Serie C of the Brazilian Championship 2014]]''' is a football competition held in Brazil, equivalent to the third division. It is contested by 20 clubs.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://int.soccerway.com/national/brazil/serie-c/2014/regular-season/r24208/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140309090902/http://int.soccerway.com/national/brazil/serie-c/2014/regular-season/r24208/|url-status=dead|archive-date=March 9, 2014|title = Summary - Serie C - Brazil - Results, fixtures, tables and news - Soccerway}}</ref>
