The 87th season of the '''[[Campeonato Gaúcho]]''' kicked off on January 20, 2007 and ended on May 6, 2007. The competition began with 18 clubs divided into two groups. Even before the end of first stage, [[Grêmio Esportivo Glória|Glória]], [[Sport Club Gaúcho|Gaúcho]], [[Esporte Clube Guarani|Guarani de Venâncio Aires]] and [[Grêmio Esportivo São José|São José de Cachoeira do Sul]] were already relegated. The two biggest clubs of [[Rio Grande do Sul]] would not meet at the final, as [[Sport Club Internacional|Internacional]] were eliminated in the first stage. Holders [[Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense|Grêmio]] won the title once more after beating [[Esporte Clube Juventude|Juventude]]. That was the 35th title in the history of the club.<ref name="rsssfbrasil">{{cite web|url=http://www.rsssfbrasil.com/tablesr/rs2007.htm|title=RSSSF – Championship of Rio Grande do Sul 2007|accessdate=August 12, 2019}}</ref><ref name="futebolnacional">{{cite web|url=https://futebolnacional.com.br/infobol/championship.jsp?code=F30267594D038BBB29B8A83A446DBD79&lang=pt_br|title=Futebolnacional.com.br – Championship of Rio Grande do Sul 2007|accessdate=August 12, 2019}}</ref>

== System ==
The championship would have four stages:<ref name="futebolnacional" /><ref name="rsssfbrasil" />

* '''First stage''': The 18 teams were divided in 2 groups of 9 teams each. They played against each other inside their groups in a double round-robin system. After 18 rounds, the top team of each group qualified to the Semifinals, the second and third-placed teams advanced to the Second round and the bottom two teams from each group were relegated to the Second Level.
* '''Second stage''': The Second and third-placed teams of each group would face each other in a single match to define the two teams that would qualify to the Semifinals.
* '''Semifinals''': The Second stage winners and the Group winners were divided in 2 groups of 2 teams each and they played in a double round-robin system. Teams with best overall record played the second leg at home.
* '''Finals''': Semifinals winners played in two matches to define the Champions. The team with best overall record played the second leg at home.
