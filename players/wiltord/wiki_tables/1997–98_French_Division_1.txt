The '''1997–98 Division 1''' was the 60th season of [[Ligue 1|Division 1]], the top French professional league for [[association football]] clubs, since its establishment in 1932. The season began on 2 August 1997, and concluded on 9 May 1998.

This season saw a change in the league format, with the number of teams reduced to 18 from 20, shortening the season from 38 to 34 rounds. As a result, four clubs which had been relegated at the end of the previous season were replaced by only two, [[LB Châteauroux|Châteauroux]] and [[Toulouse FC|Toulouse]], who entered as winners and runners up of the [[1996–97 French Division 2]]. Defending champions of the [[1996–97 French Division 1|1996–97 season]] were [[AS Monaco FC|Monaco]].

[[RC Lens|Lens]] won the championship for the first time in their 92-year history, becoming the 26th club to become [[List of French football champions|French football champions]]. Lens won their league title with 68 points, edging out second-placed [[FC Metz|Metz]] on goal difference, for whom this was their best result in history.
