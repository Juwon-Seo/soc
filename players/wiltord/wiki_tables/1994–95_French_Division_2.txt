The '''Division 2 season 1994/1995''', organised by the [[Ligue de Football Professionnel|LNF]] was won by [[Olympique de Marseille]] and saw the promotions of [[En Avant Guingamp]] and [[FC Gueugnon]], whereas [[Stade Briochin]], [[AS Beauvais]], [[CS Sedan Ardennes]] and [[Nîmes Olympique]] were relegated to [[Championnat National|National]].

==Recap==
* '''Promoted to L1''' : [[En Avant Guingamp]], [[FC Gueugnon]] ([[Olympique de Marseille]] was not promoted due to financial problems)
* '''Relegated to L2''' : [[SM Caen]], [[FC Sochaux-Montbéliard]]
