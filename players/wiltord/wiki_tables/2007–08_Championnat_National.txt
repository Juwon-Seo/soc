The '''2007–08 Championnat National''' is the 15th edition of the 3rd division league and began play in August 2007 and is slated to end in May 2008. [[Clermont Foot]], [[US Boulogne]], and [[Angers SCO]] were promoted to [[Ligue 2]], whereas [[Sporting Toulon Var|Sporting Toulon]], [[US Raon-l'Étape]], [[SO Châtellerault]], and [[AS Yzeure]] were relegated to the [[Championnat de France Amateurs|CFA]].

Promoted from the [[Championnat de France Amateurs|CFA]] were [[Calais RUFC]] from Groupe A, [[AC Arles]] from Groupe B, [[Rodez AF]] from Groupe C, and [[Villemomble Sports]] from Groupe D.
