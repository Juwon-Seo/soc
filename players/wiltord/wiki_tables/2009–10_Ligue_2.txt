The '''Ligue 2 2009–10 season''' was the sixty-ninth<ref>{{cite news |title=Palmares |url=http://www.frenchleague.com/ligue2/historique/palmares.asp |work=Ligue de Football Professionel |publisher=lfp.fr |date=18 May 2009 |access-date=26 May 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091020202212/http://www.frenchleague.com/ligue2/historique/palmares.asp |archive-date=20 October 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref> edition since its establishment. The fixtures were announced on 5 June 2009,<ref>{{cite news|title=Les calendriers 2009/2010 de Ligue 1 et Ligue 2 dévoilés |url=http://www.lfp.fr/actualiteLFP/lireArticle.asp?idArticle=13392 |work=Ligue de Football Professionel |publisher=lfp.fr |date=5 June 2009 |access-date=5 June 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090608050118/http://www.lfp.fr/actualiteLFP/lireArticle.asp?idArticle=13392 |archive-date=8 June 2009 }}</ref> and the league began on 7 August and ended on 14 May 2010.<ref>{{cite news|title=Le calendrier général de la saison 2009/2010 |url=http://www.lfp.fr/communique/lireArticle.asp?idArticle=12606 |work=Ligue de Football Professionel |publisher=lfp.fr |date=5 June 2009 |access-date=5 June 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090505060436/http://www.lfp.fr/communique/lireArticle.asp?idArticle=12606 |archive-date=5 May 2009 }}</ref>

==DNCG Ruling on Arles-Avignon==
All clubs that secured status for Ligue 2 play this season had to be approve by the [[Direction Nationale du Contrôle de Gestion|DNCG]] before becoming eligible to participate.<ref name=bye>The DNGC is responsible for overseeing the legal and financial accounts of professional and amateur football clubs in France. If clubs operating in the football leagues of France don't meet the DNGC's expectations, they can face sanctions, such as relegation.</ref><ref>{{cite news|title=LFP: DNCG |url=http://www.lfp.fr/dncg/ |work=Ligue de Football Professionel |publisher=lfp.fr |date=5 June 2009 |access-date=5 June 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090603073237/http://www.lfp.fr/dncg/ |archive-date=3 June 2009 }}</ref>

On 23 June, the DNCG ruled that [[AC Arles-Avignon]] would not be allowed to play in Ligue 2 following their promotion from the [[Championnat National]], due to irregularities in the club's financial accounts and management.<ref>{{cite news|title=Les décisions du mardi 23 juin |url=http://www.lfp.fr/dncg/lireArticle.asp?idArticle=13506 |work=Ligue de Football Professionel |publisher=lfp.fr |date=23 June 2009 |access-date=23 June 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090626104531/http://www.lfp.fr/dncg/lireArticle.asp?idArticle=13506 |archive-date=26 June 2009}}</ref> On 3 July, following an appeal, the DNCG reversed its decision reinstating Arles' Ligue 2 status.<ref>{{cite news |title=Arles-Avignon repêché par la DNCG |url=https://www.lequipe.fr/Football/breves2009/20090703_095746_arles-avignon-repeche-par-la-dncg.html |work=L'Equipe |date=3 July 2009 |access-date=3 July 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090704045228/http://www.lequipe.fr/Football/breves2009/20090703_095746_arles-avignon-repeche-par-la-dncg.html |archive-date=4 July 2009 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

|note_res_R=On 15 June 2010, following a review of each club's administrative and financial accounts in the Championnat National, the DNCG ruled that both Bastia and [[FC Gueugnon|Gueugnon]] would be relegated to the [[Championnat de France amateur]], while [[Amiens SC|Amiens]], [[En Avant de Guingamp|Guingamp]], and Strasbourg were having its accounts subjected to further deliberation to determine whether each club could retain its professional status.<ref>{{cite news |title=Les décisions de la DNCG |url=http://www.lfp.fr/dncg/lireArticle.asp?idArticle=16565 |work=[[Ligue de Football Professionnel]] |publisher=[[Ligue de Football Professionnel]] |date=15 June 2010 |access-date=15 June 2010 |language=fr |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100620174517/http://www.lfp.fr/dncg/lireArticle.asp?idArticle=16565 |archive-date=20 June 2010 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Le massacre continue |url=http://www.foot-national.com/flashes-15412.html |publisher=Foot National  |date=23 June 2010|access-date=7 July 2010| language = fr| archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20100626042359/http://www.foot-national.com/flashes-15412.html| archive-date= 26 June 2010| url-status= live}}</ref> Both Bastia and Gueugnon had the option to appeal the decision. On 25 June 2010, the [[Corsican Assembly]] and the General Council of [[Haute-Corse]] approved grants of €800,000 and €150,000 to be given to Bastia in order for the club to meet the DNCG's financial requirements, which would allow the club to remain in the Championnat National.<ref>{{cite news |title=L'assemblée de Corse sauve le club |url=http://www.foot-national.com/flashes-15460.html |publisher=Foot National |date=25 June 2010|access-date=29 June 2010| language = fr| archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20100628025341/http://www.foot-national.com/flashes-15460.html| archive-date= 28 June 2010| url-status= live}}</ref> However, on 6 July, the DNCG remained firm on its stance relegating the club to the fourth division after questioning the legitimacy of the grants and the sale of the club's training center. Bastia president, Julien Lolli, remained confident that the club would play in the Championnat National and issued an appeal to the CNOSF, the National Sporting Committee of France, the same day.<ref>{{cite news |title=Bastia en CFA |url=http://www.francefootball.fr/FF/breves2010/20100706_212811_bastia-en-cfa.html |work=[[France Football]] |publisher=[[France Football]] |date=6 July 2010 |access-date=6 July 2010 |language=fr |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100708132115/http://www.francefootball.fr/FF/breves2010/20100706_212811_bastia-en-cfa.html |archive-date=8 July 2010 |url-status=dead }}</ref> On 2 July, the DNCG announced that Gueugnon would remain in National after the club successfully appealed to the organisation.<ref>{{cite news |title=Gueugnon sauvé! |url=http://www.foot-national.com/flashes-15689.html |publisher=Foot National |date=2 July 2010|access-date=2 July 2010| language = fr| archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20100705112148/http://www.foot-national.com/flashes-15689.html| archive-date= 5 July 2010| url-status= live}}</ref> On 16 July, the CNOSF ruled against the DNCG and announced that Bastia should play in the Championnat National.<ref>{{cite news |title=Le CNOSF donne raison à Bastia |url=http://www.francefootball.fr/FF/breves2010/20100716_123406_le-cnosf-donne-raison-a-bastia.html |work=[[France Football]] |publisher=[[France Football]] |date=16 July 2010 |access-date=16 July 2010 |language=fr |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100717185418/http://www.francefootball.fr/FF/breves2010/20100716_123406_le-cnosf-donne-raison-a-bastia.html |archive-date=17 July 2010 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The club's place in the league was confirmed upon the release of the league table.

On 2 July, local media in [[Alsace]] reported that Strasbourg were on the verge of being relegated to the Championnat de France amateur by the DNCG due to financial issues.<ref>{{cite news |title=Le Racing rétrogradé en CFA  |url=http://www.dna.fr/fr/a-la-une-web/info/3422929-Football-Le-Racing-retrograde-en-CFA |work=DNA |publisher=Dernières Nouvelles d'Alsace |date=2 July 2010|access-date=7 July 2010| language = fr| archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20100705234346/http://www.dna.fr/fr/a-la-une-web/info/3422929-Football-Le-Racing-retrograde-en-CFA| archive-date= 5 July 2010| url-status= live}}</ref> The club responded by announcing its willingness to appeal if the news reported was confirmed. With the club's accounts still being reviewed, Strasbourg's financial issues were slightly alleviated after the sale of [[Striker (association football)|striker]] [[Magaye Gueye]] to English club [[Everton F.C.|Everton]] for €1.4&nbsp;million.<ref>{{cite news |title=Strasbourg fera appel si CFA |url=http://www.foot-national.com/flashes-15664.html |publisher=Foot National |date=2 July 2010|access-date=7 July 2010| language = fr| archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20100705100358/http://www.foot-national.com/flashes-15664.html| archive-date= 5 July 2010| url-status= live}}</ref> Strasbourg later transferred captain [[Guillaume Lacour]] and [[Algeria national football team|Algerian international]] [[Yacine Bezzaz]] to [[Évian Thonon Gaillard FC|Évian]] and [[Troyes AC|Troyes]], respectively, for nominal fees. On 16 July, the report was confirmed when the DNCG officially relegated Strasbourg to the CFA. Strasbourg appealed the decision the following week.<ref>{{cite news |title=Alfortville, Bastia, Colmar en National, Strasbourg en CFA|url=http://www.foot-national.com/articles-2600.html |publisher=Foot National |date=16 July 2010|access-date=16 July 2010| language = fr| archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20100717190539/http://www.foot-national.com/articles-2600.html| archive-date= 17 July 2010| url-status= live}}</ref> On 22 July, Strasbourg's appeal was successful with the DNCG ruling in favour of a return to National.<ref>{{cite news |title=Strasbourg jouera en National |url=http://www.francefootball.fr/FF/breves2010/20100722_113839_strasbourg-jouera-en-national.html |work=[[France Football]] |publisher=[[France Football]] |date=22 July 2010 |access-date=22 July 2010 |language=fr |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100722160546/http://www.francefootball.fr/FF/breves2010/20100722_113839_strasbourg-jouera-en-national.html |archive-date=22 July 2010 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The return of Strasbourg to National means that twenty-one clubs competed in the 2010–11 edition of the league.
