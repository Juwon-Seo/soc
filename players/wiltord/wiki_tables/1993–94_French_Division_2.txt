The '''Division 2 season 1993/1994''', organised by the [[Ligue de Football Professionnel|LNF]] was won by [[OGC Nice]] and saw the promotions of [[OGC Nice]], [[Stade Rennais FC]] and [[SC Bastia]], whereas [[FC Rouen]], [[US Valenciennes]], [[FC Bourges]] and [[FC Istres]] were relegated to [[Championnat National|Division 3]].
