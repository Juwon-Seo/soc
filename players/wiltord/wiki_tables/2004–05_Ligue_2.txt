The '''Ligue 2 season 2004/2005''', organised by the [[Ligue de Football Professionnel|LFP]] was won by [[AS Nancy]] and saw the promotions of [[AS Nancy]], [[Le Mans FC|Le Mans UC72]] and [[Troyes AC]], whereas [[Angers SCO]] and [[SC Bastia]] were relegated to [[Championnat National|National]].
