The '''2011–12 Ligue 2 season''' was the 73rd since its establishment.<ref>{{cite news|title=Palmarès: Les champions de France depuis 1934/1935 |url=http://new.lfp.fr/ligue2/palmares |work=[[Ligue de Football Professionnel]] |access-date=23 April 2011 |language=fr |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110419073326/http://new.lfp.fr/ligue2/palmares |archive-date=19 April 2011}}</ref> [[2010–11 Ligue 2|The previous season's champions]] was [[Evian Thonon Gaillard F.C.|Evian]]. The league schedule was announced on 31 March 2011 and the fixtures were determined on 10 June.<ref>{{cite news|title=Le calendrier dévoilé le 10 juin |url=https://www.lequipe.fr/Football/breves2011/20110530_140030_le-calendrier-devoile-le-10-juin.html |work=[[L'Equipe]] |date=30 May 2011 |access-date=30 May 2011 |language=fr |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110602131338/http://www.lequipe.fr/Football/breves2011/20110530_140030_le-calendrier-devoile-le-10-juin.html |archive-date=2 June 2011 }}</ref><ref>{{cite news|title=Les calendriers 2011/2012 |url=http://new.lfp.fr/corporate/article/les-calendriers-2011-2012.htm |work=[[Ligue de Football Professionnel]] |date=10 June 2011 |access-date=21 June 2011 |language=fr |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120119111703/http://new.lfp.fr/corporate/article/les-calendriers-2011-2012.htm |archive-date=19 January 2012 }}</ref> The season began on 29 July and ended on 18 May 2012.<ref>{{cite news |title=Le calendrier général 2011-2012 validé |url=http://new.lfp.fr/calendriers/2011_2012/calendrier_2011_2012.pdf |work=[[Ligue de Football Professionnel]] |date=31 March 2011 |access-date=22 April 2011 |language=fr |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110823041848/http://new.lfp.fr/calendriers/2011_2012/calendrier_2011_2012.pdf |archive-date=23 August 2011 |url-status=dead}}</ref> The winter break was in effect from 21 December to 14 January.

Bastia clinched the second division title on 1 May 2012 with three matches to spare after defeating [[FC Metz|Metz]] 3–0 at the [[Stade Armand Cesari]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Le SC Bastia champion! |url=http://www.lfp.fr/ligue2/article/le-sc-bastia-champion.htm |work=[[Ligue de Football Professionnel]] |date=1 May 2012 |access-date=1 May 2012 |language=fr |archive-date=9 July 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180709100021/https://www.lfp.fr/ligue2/article/le-sc-bastia-champion.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref> The title is the club's second overall in the division having won the league previously in the [[1967–68 French Division 2|1967–68 season]]. Bastia will be making its return to the first division after a seven-year absence and will be entering Ligue 1 on a run of two consecutive promotions.<ref>{{cite news |title=Bastia, sept ans de réflexion... |url=http://www.unfp.org/a-la-une/article/bastia-sept-ans-de-reflexion-02052012-6450.html |work=[[National Union of Professional Footballers]] |date=2 May 2012 |access-date=2 May 2012 |language=fr |archive-url=https://archive.today/20121228082248/http://www.unfp.org/a-la-une/article/bastia-sept-ans-de-reflexion-02052012-6450.html |archive-date=28 December 2012 |url-status=dead}}</ref> The club had earned promotion to Ligue 2 after winning the [[2010–11 Championnat National|2010–11 edition]] of the [[Championnat National]].

[[Stade de Reims|Reims]] and [[Troyes AC|Troyes]] became the second and third club, respectively, to earn promotion to Ligue 1 alongside the champions Bastia.<ref>{{cite news |title=Football: Troyes et Reims promus en Ligue 1 |url=http://www.20minutes.fr/ledirect/932999/football-troyes-reims-promus-ligue-1 |work=20 Minutes |date=11 May 2012 |access-date=11 May 2012 |language = fr}}</ref> Both clubs achieved promotion with one game to spare following league victories on 11 May 2012, which positioned each club in second and third place permanently. Reims, which is a six-time Ligue 1 champion, will be returning to the first division after over 33 years playing in the lower divisions.<ref>{{cite news |title=Football: Reims et Troyes promus en Ligue 1 |url=http://www.lemonde.fr/sport/article/2012/05/11/football-reims-et-troyes-promus-en-ligue-1_1700261_3242.html |work=[[Le Monde]]|date=11 May 2012 |access-date=11 May 2012 |language = fr}}</ref> During those 33 years, the club underwent [[liquidation]] and had all aspects of the club (its records, trophies, etc.) auctioned off. Troyes will be returning to Ligue 1 after a four-year stint in Ligue 2.<ref>{{cite news |title=Troyes une montée en Ligue 1 qui a surpris tout le monde |url=http://actu.voila.fr/actualites/documents/2012/05/11/troyes-une-montee-en-ligue-1-qui-a-surpris-tout-le-monde_2874298.html |work=Voila |date=11 May 2012 |access-date=11 May 2012 |language=fr }}{{Dead link|date=June 2023 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

There were three promoted teams from the [[Championnat National]], the third division of [[Football in France|French football]], replacing the three teams that were relegated from Ligue 2 following the [[2010–11 Ligue 2|2010–11 season]]. A total of 20 teams competed in the league with three clubs suffering relegation to the Championnat National. All clubs that secured Ligue 2 status for the season were subject to approval by the [[Direction Nationale du Contrôle de Gestion|DNCG]] before becoming eligible to participate.<ref name = dncg_rulings>The DNCG is responsible for overseeing the legal and financial accounts of professional football clubs in France. If clubs operating in [[French football]] don't meet the DNCG's expectations, they can face sanctions, such as relegation.</ref>

[[AC Arles-Avignon|Arles-Avignon]] was the first club to suffer relegation from the first division to Ligue 2. The club's impending drop occurred on 17 April 2011 following the team's 2–0 defeat to [[AS Monaco FC|AS Monaco]]. The negative result made it mathematically impossible for Arles to seize the 17th position in the table, which would have allowed the club to remain in Ligue 1. Arles-Avignon made its return to Ligue 2 after only a year's spell in the top division of French football.<ref>{{cite news |title=Arles-Avignon relégué en Ligue 2 |url=http://fr.sports.yahoo.com/17042011/1/arles-avignon-relegue-en-ligue-2.html |publisher=[[Yahoo!]] |date=17 April 2011 |access-date=23 April 2011 |language=fr }}{{Dead link|date=September 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> On 15 May, [[RC Lens|Lens]] were relegated from the first division to Ligue 2 after its 1–1 draw with Monaco. Lens returned to Ligue 2 for the first time since the [[2008–09 Ligue 2|2008–09 season]] when the club finished as champions of the league.<ref>{{cite news |title=Marseille lâche prise, Lens relégué en L2 |url=http://www.20minutes.fr/article/724295/ligue-1-marseille-lache-prise-lens-relegue-l2 |publisher=20 Minutes |date=15 May 2011 |access-date=17 May 2011 | language = fr}}</ref> On the final day of the Ligue 1 season, Monaco suffered relegation to the second division after losing 2–0 to [[Olympique Lyonnais|Lyon]]. The club's appearance in Ligue 2 was its first since 1976.<ref>{{cite news |title=Monaco relégué en L2 |url=http://www.eurosport.fr/football/ligue-1/2010-2011/monaco-relegue-en-l2_sto2814211/flashnews.shtml |publisher=[[Eurosport]] |date=29 May 2011 |access-date=29 May 2011 |language=fr |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110601011648/http://www.eurosport.fr/football/ligue-1/2010-2011/monaco-relegue-en-l2_sto2814211/flashnews.shtml |archive-date=1 June 2011 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

[[SC Bastia|Bastia]] became the first team to achieve promotion to Ligue 2 from the Championnat National after drawing 1–1 with [[Étoile Fréjus Saint-Raphaël|Fréjus Saint-Raphaël]] on 22 April. Coupled with fourth-place [[RC Strasbourg|Strasbourg]]'s draw with [[US Luzenac|Luzenac]] on the same day, the results made it mathematically impossible for the [[Alsace|Alsatians]] to catch Bastia in the standings.<ref>{{cite news |title=Bastia en Ligue 2! |url=http://www.fff.fr/champ/national/actualite/537246.shtml |publisher=[[French Football Federation]] |date=23 April 2011 |access-date=23 April 2011 |language=fr |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110426091420/http://www.fff.fr/champ/national/actualite/537246.shtml |archive-date=26 April 2011 |url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Bastia retrouve la Ligue 2 |url=https://www.lequipe.fr/Football/breves2011/20110422_222311_bastia-retrouve-la-ligue-2.html |publisher=[[L'Equipe]] |date=22 April 2011 |access-date=22 April 2011 |language=fr |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110426113139/http://www.lequipe.fr/Football/breves2011/20110422_222311_bastia-retrouve-la-ligue-2.html |archive-date=26 April 2011 |url-status=dead}}</ref> [[Amiens SC|Amiens]] achieved promotion from National to Ligue 2 on 13 May following its 2–1 home win against [[EA Guingamp|Guingamp]]. Amiens made its return to the second division after spending two seasons in the Championnat National.<ref>{{cite news |title=Amiens retrouve la L2 |url=http://www.eurosport.fr/football/national/2010-2011/amiens-retrouve-la-l2_sto2789756/flashnews.shtml |publisher=[[Eurosport]] |date=13 May 2011 |access-date=17 May 2011 |language=fr |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110516152550/http://www.eurosport.fr/football/national/2010-2011/amiens-retrouve-la-l2_sto2789756/flashnews.shtml |archive-date=16 May 2011 |url-status=dead}}</ref> Guingamp became the final club to earn promotion to National after its 2–0 away win over [[FC Rouen|Rouen]]. Similar to Bastia, Guingamp made its return to Ligue 2 after only one season in the third division.<ref>{{cite news |title=Guingamp est de retour en Ligue 2! |url=http://www.ouest-france.fr/sport/une_sport_detail_-Foot.-Guingamp-est-de-retour-en-Ligue-2-_44096-1812467_actu.Htm |publisher=Ouest-France |date=27 May 2011 |access-date=27 May 2011 | language = fr}}</ref>

=== DNCG rulings ===

On 1 July 2011, following a preliminary review of each club's administrative and financial accounts in Ligue 2, the DNCG ruled that [[Tours FC|Tours]] would be relegated to the [[Championnat National]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Tours, relégué, va faire appel |url=https://www.lequipe.fr/Football/breves2011/20110701_185150_tours-relegue-va-faire-appel.html |publisher=[[L'Equipe]] |date=1 July 2011 |access-date=1 July 2011 |language=fr |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110703075246/http://www.lequipe.fr/Football/breves2011/20110701_185150_tours-relegue-va-faire-appel.html |archive-date=3 July 2011 |url-status=dead}}</ref> Tours president, Frédéric Sebag, confirmed the demotion was as a result of the club's failure to "balance its books". Sebag also confirmed that the club would be appealing the ruling.<ref>{{cite news |title=Tours va faire appel |url=http://www.eurosport.fr/football/ligue-2/2010-2011/tours-va-faire-appel_sto2854921/flashnews.shtml |publisher=[[Eurosport]] |date=1 July 2011 |access-date=1 July 2011 |language=fr |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110729061858/http://www.eurosport.fr/football/ligue-2/2010-2011/tours-va-faire-appel_sto2854921/flashnews.shtml |archive-date=29 July 2011 |url-status=dead}}</ref> On 13 July, Tours successfully appealed to the DNCG and was subsequently reinstated into Ligue 2.<ref>{{cite news |title=L2: Tours réintégré |url=http://www.football365.fr/france/ligue-2/article_541042_L2-Tours-reintegre.shtml |publisher=Sport365 |date=13 July 2011 |access-date=13 July 2011 |language=fr |archive-date=15 July 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110715024243/http://www.football365.fr/france/ligue-2/article_541042_L2-Tours-reintegre.shtml |url-status=dead }}</ref>
