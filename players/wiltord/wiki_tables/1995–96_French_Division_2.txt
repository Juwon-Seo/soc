The '''Division 2 season 1995/1996''', organised by the [[Ligue de Football Professionnel|LNF]] was won by [[SM Caen]] and saw the promotions of [[SM Caen]], [[Olympique de Marseille]] and [[AS Nancy]], whereas [[Stade Poitevin]], [[USL Dunkerque]], [[Angers SCO]] and [[Olympique Alès]] were relegated to [[Championnat National|National]].
