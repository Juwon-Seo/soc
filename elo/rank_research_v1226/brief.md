# Opponent league-rank research (club SOC calculator)

Goal: for every item in your input CSV, find the opponent team's **final league position** in the league season that was running on the match date(s), with the number of teams, from a citable source. The calculator turns (league, season, rank, number of teams) into an opponent Elo, so the rank must be exact.

Input: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-soc/f573789f-135b-51ba-9a2c-3a06b5e8def1/scratchpad/rankfix/items_<REGION>.csv` (columns id, team, dates, current_labels, competitions, rows). `current_labels` are our existing Korean labels; they hint the country/tier but their tier/rank may be wrong. Competition codes: lg=league, dc/dk/df=domestic cup, cl*/el*/ecl*=UEFA, lib/csa=CONMEBOL, acl*/cwc etc.

Output: write `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-soc/f573789f-135b-51ba-9a2c-3a06b5e8def1/scratchpad/rankfix/out_<REGION>.csv` with exactly these columns (one row per input id, same id; if an item has dates in two different seasons, write one row per season with the same id and fill `dates` with the dates that belong to that season):
id, team, dates, country, tier, league_name, group, season, rank, n_teams, rank_type, source_url, note
- country: English country name. tier: 1 = top division, 2 = second, ... (count the national pyramid as of that season). league_name: official name that season (e.g. "Russian Premier League", "Regionalliga Nord", "Campeonato Brasileiro Série C"). group: group/conference/zone name if the division is split into parallel groups, else empty.
- season: "YYYY-YY" for leagues played across two years, "YYYY" for calendar-year leagues.
- rank: final position (integer). n_teams: number of teams in that table (the group's size for grouped divisions).
- rank_type: one of `final` (final table), `final_after_playoffs` (official final position after championship/relegation rounds or a split), `aggregate` (annual aggregate table for Apertura/Clausura-type years), `stage` (only a stage table exists; say which in note), `current` (season still in progress at 2026-11-19: give the latest standing and the date in note), `no_league` (team not in any national league that season, e.g. Brazilian club only in its state league: put the state league tier/name in note and leave rank empty unless the state league table is clear), `no_table` (division exists but no reliable table found; give tier and division).
- source_url: the page you read. note: anything special (name changes, points deductions, mid-season withdrawal, which of two same-named clubs, why the season differs from the date, etc.).

Season rule (match our calculator): cross-year leagues (Aug–May): a match from 1 Aug Y to 31 Jul Y+1 belongs to season Y-(Y+1) — so a July qualifier belongs to the season that ended that May. Calendar-year leagues (Brazil, Argentina in calendar years, MLS, Nordic/Baltic/Belarus/Kazakhstan/Ireland, Japan, Korea, China, Russia/Ukraine before their switch, etc.): the calendar year of the match. Argentina/Mexico/Chile/Paraguay/etc. short-tournament years: prefer the annual aggregate table of that year (rank_type aggregate) if one exists, else the stage table running at the match date.

Sources: Wikipedia season pages in any language (fast: `curl -s 'https://en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php?action=parse&page=2010–11_Russian_Premier_League&prop=wikitext&format=json'`; other languages similarly), RSSSF, official league/federation sites, and Transfermarkt tables (at most one Transfermarkt request at a time, ≥3.5 s apart, wait 90 s on 403/429; TM season tables for calendar-year leagues: saison_id=Y-1 shows season Y). Never use or cite zerozero, ogol, worldfootball, 11v11, fbref or sofascore, even if they show up in search results. Never bypass bot checks or Cloudflare. WebFetch may fail in subagents; use Bash curl.
